# Pass 151 — 1963 permanent successor regime after Law No.708

Date: 2026-09-25

## Result

This pass tested the prior warning that repeal of the Education Temporary Special Act (Law No.708) must not be described as restoration of the pre-1961 teacher-labor position. The successor regime is now substantially narrower and can be modeled as a split legal architecture.

First, the State Public Officials Act (Law No.1325, promulgated 1963-04-17, effective 1963-06-01) independently established Article 66's prohibition on public officials engaging in labor movements or other collective activity for matters outside public duties, subject to the de-facto-labor exception. This permanent general statute was already effective months before Law No.708's repeal took effect on 1964-01-01.

Second, the Education Public Officials Act identifies national/public school teachers as education public officials and its historical Article 43 incorporated the State Public Officials Act service regime by mutatis-mutandis reference. The exact original cross-reference range still needs transcription from the promulgated 1963 text/Gazette, so the registry records the connection while preserving that narrower documentary task.

Third, the Private School Act (Law No.1362, enacted 1963-06-26, effective 1963-07-27) created a different bridge for private-school teachers. Article 55 applies national/public teacher duty rules mutatis mutandis to private-school teachers. This does not turn private teachers into public officials. Article 58 also contains a dismissal ground involving political activity and collective refusal of classes. The consolidated legal text preserves that language, but the exact 1963 promulgated Article 58 wording remains a priority original-version check before asserting word-for-word continuity.

The result is therefore not `Law 708 repealed -> teacher labor restriction disappears`. It is better represented as `temporary cross-sector prohibition -> permanent split regime`: national/public teachers through the public-service/education-public-official track, and private teachers through the Private School Act duty/dismissal track.

## Adversarial boundary

No textual genealogy from the missing 1961-06-21 `교직단체법` has been proven. Similar vocabulary across Law No.708 Article 13, State Public Officials Act Article 66, and Private School Act teacher provisions is evidence of a related regulatory problem, not evidence that one text was copied from another. Drafts, promulgated originals, and review files are still required.

Later Constitutional Court and Supreme Court cases are being used only as `later_finding` evidence for how Article 66 was understood and applied to education public officials. They are not back-projected as evidence of the 1961 drafters' intent.
