# Run149 seventh-pass: independent US diplomatic corroboration

Five full primary FRUS texts were read: Muccio telegram 69 (1949-01-27), Muccio despatch of 1949-11-14, Butterworth memorandum of 1949-12-16, US State Department instruction of 1949-12-30, and Drumright telegram of 1950-10-28. This is an independent archival family from Korean Gazette, Assembly and newspaper records.

The January US appraisal independently identified rice procurement failures, coal transport and power output bottlenecks. February Korean mine-food funding requests and September Korean generation figures show related domestic administrative issues, but not necessarily identical underlying causes.

The November ECA despatch describes an eight-point stabilization proposal: counterpart rates, tax collection, utility charges, spending controls, accounting, vested property sales linked to land reform, distribution of aid goods and foreign-exchange auctions. Proposed rates of 500 and 600 won per dollar must not be reported as realized exchange rates. President Rhee and Prime Minister Lee Bum-suk are mentioned as participants in negotiations; this is not proof of any specific implemented order.

Important conflict: the December 16 US memorandum says the land law was promulgated June 22 and the Assembly overrode a veto June 21, while Korean Act 31 Gazette states June 21 and Korean constitutional history cites Assembly resolutions June 14-15 following a government lapse notice. The exact legal procedure and dating remain CONFLICTED, not silently reconciled.

The October 1950 Embassy account reports land reform implementation underway at invasion and translates an October 23 presidential proposal for northern areas. It is a contemporary foreign observation, not a Korean census or definitive assessment of all farmers. The FRUS editorial footnote itself corrects a referenced telegram number 249 to 245.

This cross-provenance research links land law, finance, imports, electricity, food distribution and diplomacy without equating US assessments with established Korean government intent.
