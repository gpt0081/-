# Run149 fifth-pass: agrarian, local, education and civil-service institutions

The 1949 Land Reform Act is law no.31, enacted June 21. Law no.108 is the March 10, 1950 amendment. A later constitutional-history heading incorrectly labels the 1949 original no.108, while its own narrative correctly distinguishes 31 and 108. Official law text settles the numbering. The 1949 recipient repayment formula used 12.5-hal of principal produce; the 1950 amendment changed it to equal compensation. The January 28 Assembly committee draft discussed 24-hal and eight-year payments, but the enacted statute did not adopt those values. Proposal, debate and enacted text are distinct objects.

The Local Autonomy Act no.32 was promulgated July 4, 1949 and took effect August 15. It provided presidential appointment of provincial governors and the Seoul mayor, while lower mayors were chosen by local councils. However, local council elections were delayed and first held in April-May 1952. Legal design is not the same as implemented electoral control.

The National Civil Service Act no.44 (August 12, 1949) established merit examinations, categories and appointment chains. The Education Act no.86 (December 31, 1949) provided local education committees, budgets and superintendent appointments. A text-level discrepancy remains: the original Education Act reprint ends at article 177, but a later encyclopedia describes 173 articles. The original Gazette image is the next test.

The executive's agricultural agenda was implemented through Cabinet drafts, Assembly revisions, local commissions, the civil service and financing. Neither ministerial tenure nor later political affiliation alone establishes responsibility for each clause. Source and event IDs are recorded in the fifth-pass registries.
