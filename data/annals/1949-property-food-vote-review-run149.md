# Run149 eighth-pass: revisiting inherited historical debts with original votes

April 15, 1949 food emergency bill withdrawal: the original Assembly transcript gives two distinct votes on the government's withdrawal request. First: 141 present, 46 yes, 58 no; second: 141 present, 44 yes, 68 no. The chair explicitly treated both as inconclusive and declared the withdrawal request abandoned. This is NOT a simple majority defeat recorded as final rejection. A separate debate-closure motion passed after a second vote, and must not be confused with the withdrawal vote.

Vested property: a June 3 contemporary report attributes cabinet return of the interim freeze bill to anticipated fiscal revenue and deterioration of unsold assets. The later legislative history reports June 15 override with 144 present, 132 yes, 10 no, followed by promulgation July 22 as Act 36. The June 15 roll remains to be opened. The December 3 original Assembly transcript on the distinct permanent Act 74 records 135 present, 20 yes and 49 no, insufficient to insist on the previous bill after executive reconsideration.

November 16 Assembly original debate on Article15 included competing worker, tenant and poor-household priority amendments. The final Act 74 mentions employees but does not exactly enact every proposed worker-cooperative amendment. Original participants include the chair, committee presenter, multiple sponsors and critics. This is a politician network, not a single presidential action.

The November 4 original committee explanation lists multiple party and parliamentary group consultations. The later NIKH legislative overview warns that formal Assembly party-group registration only began July 29, 1949. Therefore faction labels for earlier periods are provisional unless election registration or dated contemporaneous records support them.
