# Rhee annals supplemental: 1950 economic stabilization

Contemporaneous reporting places cabinet discussion of the fifteen stabilization principles on 1950-02-03. A later National Archives account dates announcement to 1950-03-04. The archived draft is indexed in BA0135047 item 31, pages 258-267, but the original scan was not readable in this run. Do not infer direct presidential drafting or an implementation date from the draft's existence. On 1950-03-11 the Ministry of Commerce and Industry's 50-product program and 40,000-kW requirement were reported as plans, not realized outputs. The March 25 railway-union appeal for minimum-wage legislation records advocacy, not enacted law.
