# Annals addendum, run 132

The 1948-10-25 Gazette states that the Yeosu-Suncheon martial-law decree takes effect upon promulgation. This legal date must not be conflated with earlier military announcements.

The 1949 Audit Office Decree No. 51 assigns institutional oversight and personnel procedures; it does not prove specific appointments. Food Emergency Act No. 35 temporarily suspended one grain purchase rule while retaining penalties for speculative hoarding.

For the 1960 election and protests, contemporary press, US diplomatic reports, and later Korean prosecution or judicial records are kept separate. The president's reported approval of forceful crowd-control measures is not by itself a specific order to fire live ammunition.

The compiled biography of Jeong Gwang-ho contains competing birth years 1896 and 1897, in addition to older secondary variants. Identity remains open pending original personnel and electoral records.
