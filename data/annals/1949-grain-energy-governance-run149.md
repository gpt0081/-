# Run149 sixth-pass: grain markets, trade, industry and routine governance

The April 15, 1949 Assembly original transcript records the government's March 21 Food Emergency Measures bill, March 30 committee report and April 6 request to withdraw. Agriculture Minister Lee Jong-hyeon said he had taken office March 2 and described food stock and budget constraints. These are attributed ministerial statements, not independently audited stocks.

The July 22 Food Emergency Measures Act no.35 temporarily suspended compulsory grain sales to the state until October 31, but maintained penalties for hoarding. A contemporary July 25 newspaper explanation predicted smoother market circulation; that prediction is not proof of the actual price outcome. On July 23 a different report described average official grain-price increases of about 36 percent. Their sequence does not establish causation.

The same July 22 government promulgated Vested Property Interim Measures Act no.36, barring disposal of transferred assets except farmland pending comprehensive law. This shows distinct public asset and agricultural land administration.

The September 26 Court Organization Act no.51 established district, high and Supreme Court tiers. September 22 exam rules specified a November 26 higher civil-service exam, which remains a scheduled event until results are found.

Industrial and fiscal constraints: the Commerce Ministry reportedly sought 61.8 million won for coal-mine food procurement in February; this was a funding request, not an appropriation. A September power report listed Yeongwol plant at 27.71 percent of capacity, attributing shortages partly to coal logistics. Aid distribution disputes in February involved the Commerce Ministry, ECA and private intermediaries, with press accounts that require official ECA correspondence.

Two US diplomatic telegrams dated September 29 (Muccio 1211 and Webb 817) were identified by archival metadata only. They are not counted as full documents read or as cross-verified diplomatic findings. The research graph keeps document status explicit.
