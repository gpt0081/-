# Run150 breadth: education, local administration, prosecution, land reform

Original-document-first follow-up, separate from the vested-property corrections.

- Education: law 86 enacted 1949-12-31; an accessible original-law transcription reaches article 177. Gazette image is still needed to adjudicate the competing article-count metadata. Article 5 explicitly bars using education for partisan propaganda; article 8 states six-year primary education rights. [d150b-edu86-text]
- Local administration: law 32 enacted 1949-07-04 and effective 1949-08-15. Archives history places first local council elections in April/May 1952 and identifies December 1949 postponement authority. Do not conflate enactment with electoral implementation. [d150b-local32, d150b-local-archive]
- Prosecutors: December 3, 1949 original assembly transcript records legal committee chair Baek Gwan-su explaining a bill that distinguishes ministry oversight, prosecutor hierarchy and police replacement authority. The later statute was promulgated 1949-12-20 as law 81. [d150b-prosecution-transcript, d150b-prosecution-archive]
- Land reform: law 31 (1949-06-21) is the enactment, law 108 (1950-03-10) the amendment. January 28, 1950 original transcript identifies Lee Byeong-gwan as acting industrial committee chair explaining the draft. [d150b-farm31, d150b-farm-law-history, d150b-farm-amendment]

The prosecution and land-reform transcripts are primary transcriptions. Archives interpretive pages are later secondary sources even though issued by official bodies. No unsupported personal vote, appointment or presidential instruction is inferred.
