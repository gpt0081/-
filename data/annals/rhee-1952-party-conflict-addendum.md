# 1952 party convention source conflict (run171)

The contemporary September 28 Dong-A report says the September 26 charter revision and officer elections were not completed (doc-r167-party-1952-conflict). The AKS retrospective says the party charter changed on September 26 and a central committee reform was decided September 27 (doc-r167-party-aks-retrospective). This is CONFLICTED, not silently resolved. Check original September 26 convention minutes and September 27 executive committee resolution.

The Dong-A report describes a declaration of unanimity despite some labor delegates not raising hands. This remains a press observation, not an audited vote. Lee Hwal's statement about police surveillance is an allegation, not an established order.

A July 15 Seoul civic convention report records Yun Jae-uk as moderator and 345 vice-presidential ballots, but the named candidate votes sum to 234. The remaining 111 ballots are unitemized; the cause is unknown. Source: doc-r171-nikh-19520715.
