# Run149: 1949 inspection commission and Cho Bong-am

Date: 2026-10-11. Contemporary texts opened, facsimile images not opened. Commission findings are not criminal convictions.

1949-02-02: Chair Jung In-bo reported grain-budget and fertilizer allegations to the Assembly (d149-report). An Agriculture Ministry rebuttal is indexed, but its full text is missing.

1949-02-12: Commission decision no.25 ordered disciplinary dismissal (d149-order). Nine signatures are not proof of individual votes. The original accounting vouchers remain missing.

1949-02-21: Assembly rejected arrest consent and minister resignation was accepted according to official chronology (d149-arrest). This was not an acquittal.

1949-07-08/09: A bill attributed to Lee In and 57 others sought to amend Government Organization Act articles 29 and 43. Jung publicly opposed it (d149-amendment). Bill submission is not enactment.

1949-07-22: Jung resigned. Rhee publicly attributed resignation to illness; no causal proof ties it to the bill (d149-resignation). No Jin-seol appointment appears in official chronology (d149-appointments).

1949-11-11: Seoul District Court acquitted Cho and three other defendants at first instance. Prosecutor immediately appealed (d149-judgment). The final appellate outcome is unverified. This case is separate from the 1958 Progressive Party case and 2011 retrial.

Numeric conflict: Feb 2 meal expenses 766,555.50 won vs Feb 12 766,155.50. Decision text travel cost 2,746,300 vs 3,700,000; the reported subtotal fits the first. Verify printed originals.
