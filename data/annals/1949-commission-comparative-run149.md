# Run149 third-pass: comparison of 1949 ministerial oversight cases

New direct documents: National Assembly investigation report (1949-06-03, d149-lim-assembly-inquiry); 1949-09-17 contemporary court verdict report (d149-lim-first-instance); 1951-09-18 Supreme Court final judgment report (d149-lim-supreme). The first document transcribes Assembly stenogram pages 217-221, rather than a later memoir.

The Assembly investigation had eight members divided into regional and Seoul groups. The report's presenter described five approvals, two reservations and one absent member. Its finding that the commission's notice alone did not establish grounds for dismissal is a parliamentary committee interpretation, not a court ruling.

On 1949-09-17 the trial court acquitted the commerce minister and other defendants, while convicting others; prosecution appealed. On 1951-09-18 the Supreme Court dismissed the appeal and left the minister's acquittal in place. This finality is directly supported for the commerce-minister case. A scholarly article says the separate agriculture-minister case also ended in acquittal, but its appellate original is still missing.

Conflict: the 1949 trial report states 18 defendants but its listed acquitted and convicted names appear to total 19. The June Assembly report heading prints Dangi 4272 for a March 31 notice in a 1949 proceeding, suggesting a date transcription problem. Preserve both anomalies until the originals are compared.

The two cases share a commission, prosecution and court chronology, but their actors and evidence differ. Later scholarly claims about attempted political settlement must remain attributed interpretations until letters, ministerial files and prosecution records are opened. No direct presidential order is inferred from association or tenure.
