## Corrections and corroboration
- The Korean archival chain independently confirms that 1953-08-08 was `가조인` (initialing), with Foreign Minister 변영태 and U.S. Secretary John Foster Dulles signing the initialed text at 경무대 while Rhee and Ambassador Briggs attended. It separately identifies the formal Washington signature by 변영태 and Dulles on 1953-10-01. This independently corroborates Pass 61's FRUS-based correction that August 8 and October 1 are different events.
- Presidential Archives metadata states that the treaty received Rhee's approval on 1954-01-13, unanimous consent in the Korean National Assembly plenary on 1954-01-15, and unanimous U.S. Senate consent on 1954-01-19. The restoration page further identifies an Foreign Ministry file with presidential approval 1954-01-22 and implementation 1954-01-29 concerning exchange of ratifications.
- The exhibition identifies a 1954-02-01 Presidential Secretariat record instructing the ROK ambassador in Washington to process exchange of ratifications and return the instrument/certificate. This is evidence that domestic consent did not itself equal entry into force.
- The actual exchange occurred on 1954-11-17 in Washington, associated in the exhibition with Ambassador 양유찬 and Dulles signing the Agreed Minute; legal effect followed on 1954-11-18. Therefore model at least: signature → presidential approval → National Assembly consent → U.S. Senate consent → administrative preparation → Agreed Minute/ratification exchange → entry into force.
- The exhibition explicitly links the later Agreed Minute to continued UNC operational command of ROK forces. This should remain a distinct legal/political object from the six-article defense treaty itself.
- Korean archival metadata preserves Rhee's 1953-07-07 objection that the draft lacked immediate/automatic aid and Dulles's 1953-07-25 response that U.S. action had to proceed through constitutional procedure. This independently corroborates the failed automatic-action demand found in FRUS.
- The exhibition also identifies the 1953-08-09 Rhee public address `전국민에게 보냄`. Public rhetoric treated the alliance as already politically established immediately after initialing, but this must not collapse the later legal ratification/entry-into-force stages.
