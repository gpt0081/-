### Metadata conflict found
The catalogue currently displays the M03 report item as dated **1953-05-15** while the underlying meeting is **1953-05-20**. A report cannot ordinarily precede the meeting it reports. This is therefore preserved as `CONFLICTED_METADATA` rather than silently normalised. The original cover/dispatch stamp must be inspected to determine whether the catalogue date, meeting date, or document-number association is erroneous.

## M01 actor structure newly fixed from transcript

The 1953-05-06 transcript explicitly lists:
- 홍진기 — 법무부 법무국장, **OBSERVER**.
- 지철근 — 상공부 수산국장.
- 장윤걸 — 주일대표부 3등서기관.

Japanese side includes Kubota Kanichiro, Okai Masao, Oto Shocho, Masuda Shoichi, Takahashi Satoru and Hirota Shigeru. Kubota greeted the meeting and left. This is useful attribution evidence: the first committee meeting did **not** consist of a president-to-president policy encounter; it was an interdepartmental working-level negotiation with an explicitly labelled Korean legal observer and fisheries/diplomatic officials.

This also materially strengthens three actor nodes. 지철근 is now directly present from M01 through later technical sessions, 홍진기's observer role begins at M01 rather than only the previously structured M04/M06/M08, and 장윤걸's diplomatic-staff continuity likewise begins at M01.

## Adversarial checks

1. Catalogue chronology is not assumed infallible: M03 has an impossible-looking report-before-meeting sequence and is flagged.
2. `OBSERVER` is not converted to delegate, drafter, decision-maker or approver.
3. Repeated attendance does not prove authorship of the Peace Line.
4. Fisheries bureau expertise does not prove presidential delegation of final authority.
5. A report document number does not by itself identify its drafter or signer.
6. Committee meeting date and dispatch/report date are stored separately.
7. Later 1960s compilation of 1953 proposals is not treated as identical provenance to the 1953 originals.
8. Korean catalogue and National Assembly bibliographic reproduction are cross-checks, not independent eyewitness accounts.
9. Kubota's presence at M01 is not treated as continuous participation because the transcript says he left after greetings.
10. Absence of a name from a catalogue entry is not evidence of absence from the meeting; participant attribution requires transcript or roster.
