## Material findings / corrections
- The official legal promulgation layer adds 손원일 as a named cabinet-level actor on Treaty No.34. This is not evidence that he negotiated or signed the treaty; it is evidence of his ministerial presence on the promulgation instrument. Role attribution must remain narrow.
- 변영태's role on 1954-11-18 is broader than `foreign minister`: the official promulgation text names him simultaneously as prime minister/state councilor and foreign minister. This should be distinguished from his 1953 treaty-signatory role.
- Pass 62 treated 1954-11-18 as entry into force but did not directly inspect the official law database. The official treaty page now independently confirms both effective date and gazette date as 1954-11-18.
- A provenance conflict was found: the National Archives/Google Arts metadata description says the gazette was promulgated on 1954-11-13, whereas the official National Law Information Center says 관보게재일 1954-11-18 and prints the promulgation date as 11/18. Preserve as CONFLICTED metadata until the actual gazette image/issue date is inspected.
- 양유찬's promotion blocker is materially narrowed. Multiple contemporaneous FRUS documents from 1952–1954 and Korean History DB identify him as ROK ambassador/주미대사. His exact appointment start/end and lifespan still require Korean official personnel evidence, but his role itself is independently VERIFIED.
- 양유찬 was not merely a one-day ratification-exchange signatory. The documents show him as a recurrent transmission/negotiation node between Rhee's government and the U.S. State Department, including security policy, Geneva planning, and Rhee's 1954 Washington visit.
