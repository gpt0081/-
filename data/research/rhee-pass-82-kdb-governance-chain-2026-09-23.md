## Material findings
### A. The actor chain is no longer generically `government`
The BOK institutional history provides an actor-level account that must be preserved as a sourced institutional interpretation rather than silently promoted to a primary-document fact. It identifies Finance Ministry staff as the drafting/proponent bureaucracy; 백두진 as a finance minister who was unenthusiastic about the 1951 proposal; 박희현 as the successor minister who pushed KDB enactment as a condition for Banking Act implementation; and 김유택 as the BOK governor who accepted the compromise after bank-supervision authority was transferred to BOK.

This is important adversarially: office-holding alone does not imply sponsorship. 백두진 was finance minister when the bill was introduced, yet the BOK account says he was reluctant. Conversely 박희현, who inherited the portfolio only in September 1953, appears as an enactment-stage proponent rather than the original 1951 drafter.

### B. Enactment and launch now split into additional legal/executive stages
Law No.302 was followed on 1954-01-18 by at least three distinct presidential decrees: No.858 KDB Act Enforcement Decree, No.859 liquidation of Korea Industrial Bank, and No.860 establishment of KDB. The National Archives presidential-document index independently exposes No.860 as a 1954 Legislative Office-produced presidential document.

The enforcement decree adds a concrete administrative chain: establishment registration is filed by the KDB governor; certain approvals run through the Finance Minister; the bank must take related ministries' material into account via Finance Ministry when preparing its business plan; and the Finance Minister designates the business-opening date within 30 days after establishment. This is a stronger model than treating `1954-04 launch` as a single presidential action.

### C. Cabinet review continued after 1 April launch
The National Archives Cabinet agenda index records `한국산업은행 단기4287년도 업무계획서 심의의 건` as a Finance Ministry proposal at Cabinet meeting 22 on 1954-04-17, 9 pages, archival file BA0084196. National Archives chronology also links the KDB establishment line to a 1954 business-plan review record; separate metadata identifies BG0000055 item 18-1.

Therefore the initial governance sequence includes at least: statute → Jan 18 implementing decrees → institutional establishment/registration → Apr 1 establishment → Apr 17 Cabinet review of annual business plan. The exact relationship between BG0000055 and BA0084196 must be checked rather than assumed.
