# Rhee Pass 102 — 1951 Peace Line decision-break audit

Date: 2026-09-23
Scope: Continue from Pass 101 without retrying blocked exact NAHF records. Focus on the policy break between the September 1951 cabinet-approved fisheries protection zone and the January 1952 maritime-sovereignty proclamation.

## New material finding
A KCI-indexed legal-history article, `이승만 라인(평화선)의 재고찰－해양법 발전에서의 의의와 독도 문제에서의 의미－`, exposes a source chronology that materially changes the next research question. Its bibliography/chronology identifies:
- 1951-06-18 `우리나라 연해어업 보호정책에 관한 건`, attributed as **Commerce/Industry Minister Kim Hun → Foreign Minister Byun Young-tae**.
- 1951-07-19 and 1951-08-02 letters/requests by Ambassador **Yang You-chan** to the U.S. government.
- 1951-08-10 Rusk response to Yang.
- 1951-09-07 98th temporary cabinet meeting approval of the fisheries-protection-water proposal.
- 1951-09-08 fisheries-protection-water proclamation proposal, annotated in the article's chronology as **Rhee refusal/rejection**.

The final item is important but is **not yet primary-source verified**. It is therefore a new adversarial claim, not a resolved fact. If verified, the policy chain is not a simple linear `cabinet approval -> proclamation`. It contains a presidential decision break after cabinet approval, requiring a second design stage before the 1952-01-18 proclamation.

## Independent corroboration / conflict
The official NAHF parent diplomatic-document index independently confirms the existence and dates of the underlying records: `상수 제368호` (1951-06-18), the September 4/6/8 `외정 제958호` sequence, `총의 제174호` (1951-09-08), and the wider April-August policy chain. The index alone does not expose the presidential rejection text, so the rejection claim remains OPEN.

A later official Foreign Ministry work summary (1960-09-01, `한일회담 관계 사무요약`) records the eventual 1952-01-18 proclamation as a Korean-government act, providing a downstream official anchor but not explaining the September-to-January redesign.

Kim Dong-jo's published memoir later claims that, as Foreign Ministry Political Affairs Bureau director, he advocated proclamation of a maritime-sovereignty declaration. This is first-person retrospective evidence and is valuable, but it does not independently prove authorship of the September proposal or January final text. It is stored as `later_actor_claim`, not `drafted_by`.

2. Does Kim Dong-jo memoir prove he drafted the declaration? No; retrospective self-attribution needs contemporaneous records.
3. Does `상수 제368호` prove Kim Hun personally drafted the text? No; sender attribution and drafter attribution are distinct.
4. Does Yang's July/August diplomacy prove it caused the protection-zone design? No; sequence and policy influence must be established.
5. Does the Rusk response concern only Dokdo? No; retrieve full response and Korean requests before mapping fisheries/territorial implications.
6. Does cabinet approval prove Rhee personally supported the September proposal? No.
7. Does later 1960 Foreign Ministry summary prove the 1951 rejection? No; it only anchors later official chronology.
8. Does NAHF parent-index existence equal original-text recovery? No; index-only records remain INDEX_VERIFIED.
9. Does the 1952 final declaration simply reproduce the September fisheries zone? Unknown; clause/coordinates comparison required.
10. Does a presidential refusal, if verified, imply opposition to a protection line as such? Not necessarily; the objection could concern scope, legal form, coordinates, timing, or diplomatic strategy.
