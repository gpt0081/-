## Newly verified item-level records (distinct full documents, not index)
1. FRUS XV/1 doc 162, Lightner telegram 1252, June 3 1952 19:00, https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p1/d162 . Lightner personally delivered Truman message at 11:00 and observed cabinet meeting; his account of cabinet deliberations is subsequently reported, not first-hand observation.
2. FRUS XV/1 doc 163, Lightner telegram 1259, June 3 1952 midnight, https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p1/d163 . Embassy officers reported Prime Minister Chang's account. Chang described Yi Pom-Sok's proposed arrest of all legislators, a compromise amendment, and Rhee's discussion. Nested hearsay: Chang -> embassy officers -> Lightner. Crucially Chang said he would NOT sponsor the compromise amendment; proposal does not equal formal introduction.
3. FRUS XVI doc 167, Geneva delegation telegram 84, May 17 1954, https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v16/d167 . Reproduces a 14-point DRAFT handed by Pyun to US delegates May 15. Items include UN-supervised elections, South Korean elections conditional on popular wish, census, continued ROK constitution until amended, Chinese force withdrawal, phased UN withdrawal, and UN guarantees. Draft transfer does not prove adoption.
4. FRUS XVI doc 237, Declaration by Sixteen, June 15 1954, https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v16/d237 . Signed declaration includes Y. T. Pyun for ROK, distinguishes conference final outcome from May 15 draft. Signed original transmitted under Secto 4 June 25.
