## Material advance
The release chronology is now explicitly decomposed into separate events. NIHK's constitutional-history DB points to **국회사무처 제12회 국회정기회의속기록 제57호, 1952-05-14** as the exact Korean stenographic source for the Assembly action, while FRUS document 135 independently states that the Assembly acted May 14 under Constitution Article 49. A contemporaneous Chosun Ilbo item preserved by NIHK fixes physical release on **May 19**, reporting that So left prison around 20:00, stopped at the Assembly secretariat and returned home.

That means the five-day interval is not a dating nuisance. It is an institutional event requiring its own chain: Assembly demand → prosecution/court processing → custodial execution. Later judicial-history accounts identify judge **An Yun-chul 安潤出** as ordering suspension of detention after prosecutorial non-execution, but the repository does not promote that to primary-verified fact until the original court order and prosecution/corrections papers are retrieved.

A second contemporaneous source materially expands the prosecution side. On May 10 Prosecutor General **Han Kyok-man 韓格晩** publicly characterized the shooting as a simple individual crime without political character and published a charging narrative naming Assembly Interior/Security specialist **Jeong Jin-dong 鄭鎭東** and guards **Seo Won-ryong 徐元龍, Kim Cheo-jung 金處中, Lee Jong-seok 李鍾錫**. Han now has a verified institutional-actor node; NIHK person data also preserves conflicting birth-date/year variants rather than forcing a false precision.

The April 25 Seoul Shinmun report and May 10 prosecution account disagree on details such as the reported shooting time. That conflict is now treated as evidence, not noise. Report Nos.1-3 in NARA Box 15 remain the preferred provenance-separated originals for committee findings, So Min-ho's own statement and prosecution claims.
