# Run143 findings

Four separate original legal texts were read in the official historical transcription: Law 385 (1956-02-13), Law 388 (1956-07-08), Law 501 (1958-12-26), and Law 563 (1960-11-01).

Law 385 established direct elections for city, town, and township heads, and allowed a one-month provisional budget approved by the local council. Law 388 preserved the original terms of incumbents elected before 1956-02-12. Law 501 changed the selection system to appointments but preserved the terms of incumbents. Law 563 restored direct elections including provincial governors and required delegating governments to bear the costs of delegated duties.

Four discrepancies between official transcriptions require facsimile verification: the 1960 disciplinary committee composition (five members but two-plus-two-plus-two in one transcription, versus two-plus-one-plus-two in another); the 1958 governor appointment wording; omission of town heads in one transcription of the 1960 election clause; and the 1956 effective-date verb.

These are source-text conflicts, not proven contradictions in the enacted laws. Do not silently normalize them. No claim of direct presidential instructions is supported by ministerial countersignatures.
