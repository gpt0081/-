## New verified/narrowed findings
1. Bank of Korea official archive gives a much richer actor chain than the prior statute-only node. In June 1949 the Korean government, under Finance Minister Kim Do-yeon, requested central-bank experts from the US Federal Reserve. Arthur Bloomfield and John P. Jensen arrived in September 1949, consulted Korean government/financial/ECA actors, and produced recommendations plus a draft Bank of Korea Act in February 1950. ECA legal adviser Ernst Fraenkel reviewed the draft.
2. The draft was not simply adopted. The Finance Ministry Fiscal-Financial Committee modified it, the Legislation Office convened a special committee including Finance Ministry/Legislation Office/Bank of Chosen actors, cabinet passed the bill on 1950-03-14, it went to the National Assembly on 1950-03-18, and plenary debate occurred on April 18 and 21. The BOK archive records passage with 102 present, 78 yes, 6 no. Member-level votes remain open.
3. Presidential Decree No.350 on 1950-05-11 created the Bank of Korea Establishment Committee. Finance Minister Choi Sun-ju chaired it and Bank of Chosen Governor Gu Yong-seo was vice-chair. The committee operated by majority decision, so chairmanship is not treated as unilateral authorship.
4. Gu Yong-seo became first Bank of Korea governor on 1950-06-05 and the first Monetary Policy Board met that day. This supports an event chain separating legal design, legislative adoption, establishment implementation, and operation.
5. In 1952 the Bank of Korea document bureau sent Bloomfield forty questions about interpretation/operation of the Bank of Korea and Banking Acts, including board-versus-governor authority. This is a concrete lead for testing whether the formal independence design survived implementation.
6. National Archives identifies the original Trade Union Act No.280 in Gazette No.849, record BA0186206, eight pages, and the April 20 implementing decree/rules in BA0185648. This upgrades the labor-law track from law-history metadata toward page-level archival targets.
7. AKS labor-history material states Jeon Jin-han submitted an emergency motion in December 1952 to prioritize deliberation of the Trade Union, Labor Committee and Labor Dispute Adjustment bills, and that the motion passed. This is not yet treated as primary proof; the exact National Assembly stenographic record is queued.
8. The labor-law package should be represented as at least four linked statutes/actions: Labor Dispute Adjustment Act No.279, Trade Union Act No.280, Labor Committee Act No.281, and Labor Standards Act No.286, rather than the two-law shorthand used in Pass 260.
9. A metadata conflict was detected for Labor Standards Act No.286. Pass 260 carried 1953-05-10. A National Archives gazette search exposes a May 10 item but labels it '법률제28호', while the NIHK constitutional-history table gives Act No.286 as 1953-05-15. This remains CONFLICTED pending inspection of Gazette No.890 original images and the official law-history record. No silent overwrite.
10. NEC's election-history exhibit confirms the first city/town/village council election on 1952-04-25 and first provincial council election on 1952-05-10. Contemporary NIHK compilations report 1,066 candidates and 378 elected for 18 city councils, including 118 Liberty Party and 148 independent winners. Those numbers are city-council figures, not safe nationwide totals for all city/town/village councils; Pass 261 records this scope correction.
