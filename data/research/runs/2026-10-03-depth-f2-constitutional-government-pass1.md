## Key findings
1. The Constitution states that it was enacted by the National Assembly on 1948-07-12 and was promulgated on 07-17. The research model therefore treats constitution-making as an assembly process, not a single-person authorship claim.
2. The Government Organization Act was under active article-by-article Assembly debate, including rejected proposals for a separate health/welfare ministry and independent security ministry. This requires actor-level reconstruction of institutional design.
3. On 1948-07-20 the Assembly elected 이승만 president (180/196 present votes) and 이시영 vice president on the second ballot. This is a distinct legislative-election action, not a popular presidential election.
4. After the presidency vacated the speakership, 신익희 was elected speaker and 김약수 vice speaker on 1948-08-04, demonstrating an institutional succession chain independent of the executive.
5. 이범석's premiership required Assembly approval; contemporary records report 110 votes for and 84 against. Cabinet formation should therefore be modeled as nomination/approval/appointment rather than a presidential list alone.
6. The first cabinet included 김도연, 조봉암, 이인, 안호상, 임영신, 전진한, 윤치영 and others. These become independent policy nodes for finance, agriculture, justice, education, industry, social affairs and interior/security.
7. 김병로's chief-justice appointment was approved by the Assembly on 1948-08-05. Judiciary formation is therefore a separate institutional chain.
8. Government establishment on 08-15, USAMGIK termination/administrative takeover around 08-16, and later completion of transfer arrangements are not collapsed into one instant.
9. The Anti-National Activities Punishment Act was an Assembly legislative product under Constitution Article 101, passed before presidential promulgation as Law No.3 on 09-22. This prevents falsely attributing the entire purge policy to either president or legislature alone.
10. The Agriculture Ministry submitted a detailed farmland-reform draft on 1948-11-23, while separate emergency agricultural appropriations were being prepared. This adds ordinary governance and budget administration to the political graph.
