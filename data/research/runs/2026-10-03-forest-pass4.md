## Key source findings used to seed gaps
1. National Archives' independence-movement collection explicitly describes the Provisional Government's Yeontong system as a domestic connection and fund-raising channel, and records independence bonds and regular overseas Korean contributions as government revenue.
2. An archival colonial court record CJA0000261 describes contacts with the Shanghai Provisional Government and plans to collect independence-movement funds; this provides an item-level bridge for F0 legal/finance cells without treating colonial judgments as neutral truth about defendants' political intent.
3. The Political Activity Purification Act is preserved in the official statute-history database; it anchors the post-5·16 political exclusion/transitional-justice cell.
4. Constitutional Court statistics preserve a long series of National Assembly member/Speaker competence-dispute cases, providing a non-presidential institutional line for post-1987 constitutional conflict.
5. The Supreme Court case-law database identifies 2017Do14322 as a Public Official Election Act/National Intelligence Service Act case, anchoring the 2008-16 intelligence/judicial cells.
6. The 2020 full revision of the National Intelligence Service Act explicitly states political neutrality, clearer duties, stronger National Assembly control, and prevention of domestic political intervention as reform purposes.
7. The Truth and Reconciliation Framework Act was fully revised in 2026, explicitly reconstituting a third commission and broadening/strengthening investigation architecture; this is stored as a later institutional development, not retroactively projected onto earlier periods.
8. NEC's official open-data portal exposes election datasets, including the 2025 21st presidential election, allowing F10 electoral-cleavage work to be normalized against official results rather than media summaries.

## Adversarial propositions retained
1. Yeontong was a connection/fund-raising system ≠ proof that every domestic fund transfer was centrally directed.
2. Colonial court judgments document state allegations/findings ≠ neutral proof of defendants' subjective motives.
3. Political Activity Purification Act existence ≠ proof of identical application to every restricted politician.
4. Emergency-decree era conviction ≠ later constitutional legitimacy; contemporary judgment and later review must be separate.
5. Korea-gate evidence from US sources ≠ automatic proof of every Korean actor's knowledge or direction.
6. 5·18 US diplomatic observation ≠ Korean legal responsibility finding.
7. NIS political-intervention convictions ≠ proof that every intelligence activity in the era was partisan.
8. COVID central-local policy disagreement ≠ proof of legal authority violation.
9. Contemporary F10 allegation/indictment ≠ final judicial finding.
10. Election demographic analysis ≠ official result unless derived from NEC data; survey inference remains a separate evidence type.
