That investigator must not be silently merged with the Gangwon National Assembly commissioner 李鍾淳. Because the same hanja is also used by the commissioner, this is a separate homonym-sensitive node and must not be treated as the commissioner's own disposition without address/occupation/case-file identity evidence.

## Adversarial result / correction to pass 194
Pass 194 framed the June resignation versus June/August opinion production as a live three-way conflict. That framing is now **narrowed**: later August committee-member activity is expected because Lee Jong-sun was re-elected to the reconstituted commission. The remaining uncertainty is not whether resignation automatically ended all later service, but (a) exact date and parliamentary record of the re-election, (b) whether the 20 Jun opinion preceded or followed the formal reconstitution, and (c) distinguishing the Gangwon commissioner from the Gyeonggi investigator and the same-hanja non-prosecution subject.

This is a correction-by-addition, not silent overwrite: the earlier conflict is retained as research history, while its mechanism is revised.
