## Contradiction/uncertainty highlights
- FRUS 488 (Sept 2 transmission) explicitly dates Muccio/Rhee/Noble conversation **Sept 1**, while a Korean translated display for the same document says **Aug 1**. This is a translation/transcription conflict, not a historical date correction silently applied.
- FRUS 488 describes a parliamentary dismissal resolution already adopted, while FRUS 504 identifies an overwhelming vote on **Sept 6**. These may refer to different procedural stages; parliamentary original minutes needed.
- FRUS 529 contains Defense Minister Shin Sung-mo's categorical denial of unlawful executions. The independent official 2010 truth commission findings for Daejeon San-nae document killings without due process. The denial is recorded as his statement, not adopted as fact; no individual presidential command inferred.
- FRUS 491/509 predict or warn Chinese entry, while 508/523/535 convey skepticism or no-intervention expectations. Each is an intelligence report with separate source quality, not a retrospective fact.
- FRUS 486 is NSC 81 **draft**, FRUS 500 records JCS objection, FRUS 505 is NSC 81/1 policy report. Drafts and approvals are separate.
