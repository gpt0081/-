# Run166c additional research | May-June 1950

20 additional distinct FRUS original-document transcriptions were read directly (documents 34-53). **Cumulative run166: 47** distinct meaningful contemporary texts, no original-page image. All 20 are US FRUS provenance, not independent source families. 1950-03-15 Korean Assembly original and UN A/1350 full report remain inaccessible.

1. **Military aid was proposed, not delivered:** May 10 State/Defense meeting discussed 20-40 F-51 fighters and policy/funding constraints; May 23 mission recommended 40 F-51, 10 T-6 and 3 C-47 aircraft with training and maintenance. Actual transfer is unverified. FRUS d36, d37, d40, d41.
2. **Competing threat estimates:** May 10 ROK Defense Ministry publicly estimated northern force 183,100 and 173 tanks. May 11 US Embassy estimated 103,000 troops and 65 tanks. Distinct categories, methods and intentions remain to be audited; neither estimate is ground truth. FRUS d38, d39.
3. **Election freedom was not uniform:** May 27 US Embassy reported generally free campaign conditions but also about 15 government arrests and allegations of harassment of some moderate candidates. This is an observer report, not a judicial finding. FRUS d43.
4. **UN contact does not equal endorsement:** June 7 northern political coalition proposed August elections without UNCOK participation. June 10 UNCOK representative met northern envoys near Yohyun. June 11-12 three envoys were reportedly detained; final disposition unverified. FRUS d46-d51.
5. **Military comparison scope correction:** June 13 Washington challenged a report saying the South Korean army was superior; June 14 Muccio clarified he meant infantry training, leadership and small arms, not air power, tanks or heavy artillery. FRUS d52-d53.
6. **Peace-time administration:** May 29 KMAG circular describes logistics accounting and fuel/vehicle conservation reforms; implementation and effectiveness still require Korean original ledgers. FRUS d44.
