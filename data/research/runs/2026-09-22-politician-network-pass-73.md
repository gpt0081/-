Korean archival records show an active September–December phase. The data model should therefore distinguish August UNGA voting from later conference-organization diplomacy.

The pass also adversarially checked attribution. `le_005_3180` is an Oliver letter that indexes Pyun Yung-tai, Dulles, Eisenhower and Yang You-chan; it is not evidence that Pyun authored the position. Similarly, the archive's placement inside the Rhee correspondence collection does not make every document a Rhee-authored instruction.
