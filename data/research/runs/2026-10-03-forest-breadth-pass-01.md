## Concrete findings used for seeding
- The National Institute of Korean History constitutional-history database spans the 1948 constitution through all nine constitutional amendments, providing a non-presidential constitutional backbone.
- The 1961 coup dissolved the bicameral National Assembly and local councils and the Supreme Council became the top governing institution; party activity was subsequently suppressed before civilian-transition party formation.
- The Democratic Republican Party was founded on 1963-02-26 with Jeong Gu-yeong as president and Kim Jeong-ryeol as party chair according to the contemporary chronology entry.
- The 1963 presidential election record provides candidate and regional vote data, useful for separating electoral structure from presidential biography.
- The 1972 Yushin constitutional campaign included Prime Minister Kim Jong-pil publicly advocating confirmation of the proposed constitution, illustrating non-president actor attribution.
- On 1980-05-18 Martial Law Command Proclamation No. 10 halted political activity and closed universities; this is seeded separately from later 5·18 findings.
- The 1987-06-29 declaration, subsequent party negotiations, and 1987-10-27 referendum form a multi-actor constitutional chain rather than a single-person event.
- The 1987 presidential election and 1988 National Assembly election provide a baseline for regional electoral cleavage and the emergence of a divided legislature.
- The 1990 Democratic Liberal Party formation joined the Democratic Justice Party, Reunification Democratic Party, and New Democratic Republican Party; the later leaked three-leader memorandum is a separate intra-party conflict lead.
- The 1993 financial real-name system was implemented by presidential emergency economic order and must be connected to legislative approval and prior failed implementation attempts rather than stored as a slogan.
- Later legal treatment of 12·12 and 5·18 changed over time: a 1995 prosecution non-indictment position was followed by trials and a 1996 appellate judgment. These are stored as temporally distinct official findings, not silently reconciled.
- IMF-era restructuring connected government, major business groups, labor-market reform, public-sector restructuring, and social safety-net policy, requiring cross-layer treatment.
- The 2000 inter-Korean summit links executive diplomacy, foreign-policy bureaucracy, business actors and inter-Korean economic projects.
- The 2004 impeachment sequence links party/legislative action, civil mobilization, Constitutional Court adjudication, and the subsequent parliamentary election.
- Constitutional Court statistics establish three presidential impeachment outcomes as distinct institutional precedents: 2004 dismissal, 2017 removal, 2025 removal.

## Adversarial checks
At least ten proposition-level checks were framed against over-simple narratives:
1. 1987 democratization was not treated as solely the 6·29 declaration; party negotiation, civic mobilization and referendum are separate causal layers.
2. 1987 direct election did not automatically yield unified democratic-opposition victory; the four-candidate result is preserved.
3. 1990 party merger is not equated with stable ideological unification; the internal constitutional memorandum conflict is preserved.
4. Financial real-name reform is not attributed only to one presidential decision; earlier statutory history and legislative approval remain leads.
5. Local autonomy restoration is not dated from a single law alone; council and mayor/governor election restoration are separated.
6. 5·18 legal responsibility is not frozen at the 1995 prosecution position; later trials are separate later findings.
7. IMF restructuring is not represented as only an IMF-government relation; chaebol, labor and public-sector actors are separate nodes.
8. The 2000 summit is not reduced to two leaders; bureaucratic, international and business channels remain leads.
9. The 2004 impeachment is not represented only as a presidential event; National Assembly, Constitutional Court and civic action are independent institutional actors.
10. The 2017 and 2025 removals are not assumed to have identical legal grounds merely because both resulted in removal; each judgment must remain a separate claim/evidence set.
