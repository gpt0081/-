## Material advance
The largest identity gap from pass273 is resolved. The National Institute of Korean History's Korean rendering of the FRUS editorial note explicitly renders `Yun-Kyong` as **윤우경**. The Academy of Korean Studies independently identifies **윤우경(尹宇景, 1900-1995)** as Seoul police chief and then Interior Ministry Chief of National Police in 1952. This is strong enough for a migration-ready stable candidate `pol-yun-woo-kyong-1900`; the remaining question is the exact personnel order/effective-date instrument, not identity.

The same pass resolves `Hong Pom-Hi` to **홍범희** and adds a biographical layer from AKS: 洪範憙, 1917-1972, former Constituent Assembly member, twice Interior Vice Minister, later Freedom Party central committee member. A Hanja variant appears in later office lists, so the candidate preserves the AKS form and explicitly leaves primary personnel-gazette spelling as a verification lead.

The command-chain finding is more consequential. FRUS telegram 1170, reporting Lightner and Van Fleet's May 27 meeting with Rhee, records Rhee saying that the cabinet had decided on martial law toward the end of the prior week; when his approval was requested he agreed and personally suggested including Pusan. Rhee said that implementation was urgent, that he telephoned Army Chief Lee Jong-chan, that the Defense Minister would issue the decree, Won Yong-deok would be appointed martial-law commander, and Won would take orders directly from the President. This is not a recovered signed Korean order, but it is a contemporaneous diplomatic record of Rhee's own stated arrangement. It materially narrows the political/command authority chain.

The same document supplies adversarial evidence against over-attributing Lee Jong-chan. Van Fleet said neither he nor Lee had been informed or consulted **in advance of the decision** to impose martial law. The best current model is therefore sequential: cabinet/presidential political decision first, later contact with Lee for implementation, with a subsequent command dispute. Rhee showed Van Fleet papers he characterized as Lee's messages to Won and other officers that countermanded their oral understanding and required the martial-law commander to act only on Lee's instructions. Those papers are not reproduced. They are now among the highest-value missing originals in the repository.

This sharpens `HD-RHEE-006` without falsely resolving it. There is now much stronger evidence that Rhee claimed direct authority over Won for the martial-law regime and that Rhee personally proposed Pusan's inclusion. There is still no recovered individual arrest order proving that Rhee personally ordered So Min-ho's arrest, the May 26 bus detention, or each opposition legislator arrest. Political authority, command arrangement and individual coercive acts remain separate claims.

A/2187 adds an independent UN provenance line for So Min-ho: after martial law on May 25, ROK military police arrested him and his case was transferred to court martial; the report reproduces a public notice attributed to the Martial Law Commander. The UN PDF itself was indexed and text surfaced, but direct PDF open returned 403 in this environment, so this pass records that access limitation rather than claiming a full page-by-page PDF read.

FRUS telegram 1200 also fixes the May 30 US institutional position: Lightner formally told Rhee that Pusan martial law should be lifted because CG EUSAK judged it unnecessary as a military necessity. Rhee said it would be lifted shortly while defending action against an alleged internal conspiracy. The repository therefore retains two contemporaneous positions rather than collapsing them: Korean executive internal-security justification versus US/UNC military-necessity objection.

Finally, FRUS d206, citing A/2187, separates the later July 2-4 police coercion of legislators from the May 26 bus detention. Police were ordered to 'guide and escort' absent Assembly members to the special session; the July 4 compromise amendment was adopted 163-0 with 3 abstentions among 166. This later event gets its own event line.
