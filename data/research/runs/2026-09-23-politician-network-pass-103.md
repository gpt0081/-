## Main finding
The official NAHF archival item `kj.d_0007_0010_0590` was recovered as text. It verifies that on 1951-09-08 the Foreign Minister submitted `외정 제958호` to the President after the 98th temporary cabinet meeting had passed the fisheries-protection-zone proposal `원안대로`. The proposal specifically contemplated a **presidential declaration**, proving that cabinet approval and presidential proclamation were separate procedural stages.

The primary transcription does **not** itself show an explicit presidential rejection. Later sources conflict on the exact disposition: a KCI-indexed chronology says `이승만 거부`, while a Korea Maritime Foundation / marine-education chronology says `대통령 결재 보류`. Therefore the former OPEN claim has been narrowed to a **CONFLICTED presidential disposition**: non-progression at the September stage is supported, but reject vs hold/withhold cannot yet be assigned as a primary-source fact.

The institutional chronology also supplies a sharper downstream target: 1952-01-15, fourth cabinet meeting, where the broader `인접해양에 대한 주권에 관한 선언` was reportedly urgently tabled and passed before the 1/18 proclamation. This makes direct September-vs-January text/coordinate comparison the next depth-first task.
