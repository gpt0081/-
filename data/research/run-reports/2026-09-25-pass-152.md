# Pass 152 — private-teacher service bridge / Law 708 repeal audit

## Material advance

The principal open question inherited from Pass 151 was the exact scope of former Education Public Officials Act Article 43. An official Ministry of Government Legislation interpretation (11-0492, 2011-10-27) reconstructs the historical chain explicitly: former Article 43 applied State Public Officials Act Articles 53 through 67 mutatis mutandis to education public officials. Article 66 therefore sat inside the incorporated range. The same interpretation states that Private School Act Article 55 has remained unchanged since Law No.1362 was enacted on 1963-06-26 and explains Article 55 as the bridge by which private-school teacher service rules reached the public-teacher regime.

This narrows the successor architecture to:

`Private School Act Art.55 -> former Education Public Officials Act Art.43 -> State Public Officials Act Arts.53-67 (including Art.66)`

This is a legal service-rule bridge, not a declaration that private-school teachers were public officials. The interpretation also treats Private School Act Art.58(1)(4), including collective refusal of classes, as a distinct dismissal mechanism. It must remain separate from Article 66 in the event/claim graph.

A second primary-law advance pins Law No.1466. The official amendment text expressly repealed Law No.708 effective 1964-01-01. The official repeal reason states that the temporary law had been enacted to restore educational administrative order after May 16 and that the government considered normal order largely restored, eliminating practical need for the temporary statute. That statement is preserved as an institutional rationale, not converted into an independent factual finding about conditions in education.

## Uncertainty retained

No evidence yet proves textual descent from the missing 1961-06-21 Teachers Organization Bill into Law No.708 Art.13, State Public Officials Act Art.66, or Private School Act Arts.55/58. Functional and legal continuity are now much stronger than Pass 151, but textual genealogy remains OPEN.

The 1963/64 promulgated Education Public Officials Act text should still be obtained and Article 43 transcribed verbatim even though the later official interpretation supplies the exact incorporated range. Original Gazette versions of Private School Act Arts.55 and 58 remain priority pins.
