## Material findings

2. Local autonomy is no longer represented merely by promulgation. The NIKH constitutional-history material identifies a 1949-03-31 government reconsideration request and an Apr.1 Assembly reconsideration fight, including the government's concern about immediate full elections and elected governors with police authority. June deliberation records show further legislative actor participation.
3. Ahn Ho-sang is upgraded to VERIFIED in the pass graph because an official chronology directly attributes the nationwide teacher ideological screening/removal action to the education minister, while the NIKH person index independently anchors identity. Presidential/cabinet authorization remains OPEN.
4. Geochang accountability was decomposed further. Indictment, witness examination, police-selection testimony, sentence chronology, and the military court's stated reasons are separate documentary objects. A police witness record states that police screened roughly 400 residents and selected 187, while the same hearing records former Defense Minister Shin Sung-mo's post-event visit. Neither fact alone proves the initial massacre command chain.
5. Kim Yun-keun is raised from DISCOVERED to IDENTIFIED because the official chronology explicitly names him as National Defense Corps commander at arrest on 1951-05-17. The financial and supervisory responsibility chain remains to be established from the Assembly investigation and court judgment.
6. The 1959 Progressive Party court record remains adversarially important: the court account says the party platform and peaceful-unification advocacy were not themselves unconstitutional, which must be separated from the judgment against Cho Bong-am personally.
7. The 3·15 election aftermath now has three distinct later judicial stages registered: Apr.17 ordinary-court sentencing, Sep.20 military-revolution trial, and Dec.6 appeal. These must not be collapsed into one later finding.
