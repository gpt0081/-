## Material findings
1. Three separate 1949 presidential statements are now treated as distinct evidence for Rhee's public policy toward enforcement of the Anti-National Activities Punishment Act: Jan.10 minimization, Feb.2 limits on arrests, Feb.15 special-police/law-amendment position. They do not by themselves prove the later police operational command chain.
2. Education gained an independent ministerial actor: Education Minister Ahn Ho-sang is recorded in the official chronology as conducting ideological screening of teachers and removal of roughly 5,000. Presidential/cabinet authorization remains an open question.
3. Land reform gained a hard Apr.27, 1949 National Assembly passage anchor plus Agriculture Ministry explanatory material. This is deliberately not converted into a sole-authorship claim for either Rhee or Cho Bong-am.
4. Local administration gained the Jul.2, 1949 presidential promulgation anchor for the Local Autonomy Act. Drafting, implementation delay and the 1952 local elections remain separate research actions.
5. The Geochang chain gained indictment, courtroom witness, and military-judgment materials. These records expose several layers that must not be collapsed: field perpetrators, police selection/testimony, military investigation, ministerial accountability, prosecution, and judgment.
6. Paek Tu-chin is strengthened as an independent economic-policy actor. His Jul.8, 1953 signed letter shows substantive negotiation over exchange rate and aid arrangements, while an Aug.6 FRUS meeting records him alongside Defense Minister Son Won-il and service chiefs Paik Sun-yup, Park Ok-kyu and Choi Yong-duk.
7. The 1956 election result anchors Rhee, Cho Bong-am and Shin Ik-hui in one electoral event but also warns against reducing the contest to two surviving candidates.
8. The 1958 Progressive Party deregistration announcement is stored as a government claim. The 1959 Supreme Court summary is adversarially important because it states that the party platform and peaceful-unification advocacy were not themselves unconstitutional while reaching different conclusions on Cho Bong-am personally.
9. The 1960 official election chronology explicitly notes cancellation due to fraud. Therefore the published totals are evidence of the administered count, not a valid democratic result.
10. The Apr.17, 1961 later judgment naming Choi In-kyu and other election officials is a later judicial finding and is not inflated into proof of every alleged presidential instruction.
