## Finding A · Law No.501 is now a concrete statute node, not just a Dec.24 side-note
The Presidential Archives policy index explicitly lists a 1958 `지방자치법중개정법률` under Rhee-era local-autonomy legislation. AKS dates parliamentary passage to 1958-12-24 and promulgation to 1958-12-26 as Law No.501. A recovered statute text mirror likewise identifies `[시행 1958.12.26.] [법률 제501호, 1958.12.26., 일부개정]`.

The recovered Article 98 text is structurally important: provincial governors and the Seoul mayor were presidential appointees; city mayors were appointed by the President on governor recommendation through the Interior Minister; eup/myeon heads were appointed by the governor on county-head recommendation. This gives the event graph an explicit appointment chain instead of the vague phrase `local heads became appointive`.

The statute mirror is not treated as equivalent to an official Gazette image. The next pass should recover the official promulgated text from 국가법령정보센터/관보 or the Presidential Archives original viewer and then replace/upgrade the document provenance.

## Finding B · the amendment had a wider institutional payload than appointment alone
AKS identifies additional changes: closure authority when councils exceeded statutory sitting days, abolition of committee meetings while councils were closed, extension of local-council terms, appointment of ri/dong heads, reorganization of lower neighborhood units, and abolition of no-confidence against council leadership. Therefore the Dec.24 local-autonomy subevent should not be modeled solely as an electoral-rule change; it also shifted council procedure and central/provincial supervisory power.

This widens the actor graph toward Interior Ministry officials, the Assembly Interior Committee, governors/county heads, and local elected politicians affected by the transition.

## Finding C · the 1960 reversal supplies a useful falsification/comparison edge
The KHDB constitutional-history material describes the 1958 change as presidential appointment of governors/Seoul mayor/city mayors and gubernatorial appointment of eup/myeon heads, then notes restoration of direct election for city/eup/myeon heads in the Third Constitutional Amendment. The statute history also shows a new Local Autonomy Law amendment, Law No.563, on 1960-11-01.

This is important because the graph can represent `1958 centralization → 1960 constitutional/statutory reversal` as separate institutional decisions rather than treating the 1958 rule as a permanent administrative condition.
