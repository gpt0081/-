NIKH's chronology records the Dec.24 passage under invocation of parliamentary security/order authority; the Gazette anchors the later promulgated law. This avoids collapsing legislative procedure and legal promulgation into one event.
6. NIKH also preserves a same-day opposition committee condemnation. It is retained as an actor claim, not neutral fact. The coercive-procedure proposition is separately checked against the NIKH contemporaneous material bundle and the already registered U.S. diplomatic records.
