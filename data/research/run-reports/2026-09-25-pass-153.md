# Pass 153 — original 1963 teacher labor-control text audit

## Material advance

This pass found a temporal-text problem worth correcting rather than papering over. The original 1963 Private School Act (Law No.1362) Article 58(1)(4) did not merely list political activity, collective class refusal and partisan student agitation. The original clause expressly included **노동운동** as a dismissal ground. The original transcription reads: `정치운동 또는 노동운동을 하거나 집단적으로 수업을 거부하거나 또는 어느 정당을 지지 또는 반대하기 위하여 학생을 지도ㆍ선동한 때`.

That matters because Pass 152 summarized a later official interpretation's description of Article 58 without preserving the original 1963 temporal wording. The repository must therefore keep amendment layers separate. This is a correction of temporal precision, not a reversal of the earlier structural finding.

The structural picture is now stronger: the permanent 1963 regime had **two distinct private-teacher control paths**. Article 55 incorporated national/public-school teacher service rules. Separately, Article 58(1)(4) itself expressly supplied a private-school dismissal ground naming labor movement. Article 58(2) required disciplinary-committee consent for dismissal under grounds 2 through 5, so the consequence should not be modeled as automatic dismissal.

The historical Law No.1325 State Public Officials Act Article 66 text was also pinned: it prohibited `노동운동 기타 공무 이외의 일을 위한 집단적행위`, subject to the de facto labor-worker exception. The 1964 Education Public Officials Act version confirms Article 43's title and placement as `국가공무원법의 준용`; the web rendering suppresses the incorporated-number range, so the exact 53-67 range still rests on official MOLEG interpretation 11-0492 until the Gazette image is obtained.

## Uncertainty retained

None of this proves textual descent from the missing 1961-06-21 Teachers Organization Bill or Law No.708 Article 13. Functional overlap is increasingly clear, but clause genealogy remains OPEN. The original Gazette images for Laws 1325, 1362 and 1463 remain the decisive primary-image targets.

A new priority is to identify the amendment that removed `노동운동` from Private School Act Article 58(1)(4), and to test whether that deletion was coordinated with later teacher-union/labor legislation.
