## Material findings

1. **A near-contemporaneous source for the first decade has been located.** Park Dong-seo's 1969 article `교육내용` in SNU GSPA's own `행정논총` explicitly says it reviews how graduate students were educated during the preceding ten years and divides the inquiry into admissions, curriculum and student guidance. This is now the highest-yield text target for reconstructing the 1959 intake before the original admission register is recovered. The repository exposes a 0.65 MB PDF, but this pass did not obtain its internal tables, so no statistics beyond the repository abstract are asserted.

2. **The first cohort now has multiple independently anchored named candidates.** SNU's official history continues to bound the population at 80 first MPA graduates in March 1961. Oh Seok-hong's first-person 2009 autobiographical essay says he entered when GSPA was founded and became a member of its first graduating class. Korea University's official profile independently dates Paik Wan-ki's SNU undergraduate graduation to 1959 and GSPA MPA to 1961. Kang Shin-taek biographies likewise give 1959 SNU Political Science graduation and a 1961 SNU public-administration master's degree. Only Oh is explicitly first-cohort by first-person testimony in the material inspected here; Paik and Kang remain strong candidates pending the register.

3. **The No Yung-hee anomaly is real and should not be normalized away.** KRIHS's official 2021 exhibition booklet gives No Yung-hee's SNU GSPA professorship as `1958년 8월 ~ 1973년 4월`. This is months earlier than GSPA's January 1959 institutional founding convention and the April 1959 general effective date previously recovered for Presidential Decree No.1429. The discrepancy therefore exists in an official institutional biography, not merely in a tertiary snippet.

4. **A Law College trace strengthens, but does not prove, a transfer/retrospective-label hypothesis.** SNU's repository holds a 1960 No Yung-hee review in `법학`, published by the SNU Law Research Institute and classified under the College of Law/Law School collection. One plausible explanation is that a pre-GSPA Law College appointment was later represented as a continuous GSPA career. That remains a hypothesis until the August 1958 personnel order and 1958-1959 faculty rosters are recovered.

5. **Founding faculty should remain distinct from politician nodes.** A 2026 Yonhap obituary describes Yu Hun as a founding GSPA professor serving from 1959, and records his 1953 fifth higher civil-service examination success. That is relevant to the state-administration training network but does not by itself establish political-office status, decree authorship or policy origination. The politician registry was therefore not padded with academic names.
