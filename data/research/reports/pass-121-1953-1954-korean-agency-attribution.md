# Pass 121 — 1953–1954 Korean agency and attribution graph

Date: 2026-09-24
Scope: Rhee backbone with independent non-presidential actor expansion. Floot untouched.

## Research result

This pass deliberately moved from attendance lists to attributable Korean actions in the 1953 Dulles consultations and 1954 Washington talks.

### 1. Economic aid and CEB, 6 August 1953
FRUS XV/2 doc. 737 records ROK participants Rhee Syngman, Prime Minister Paik Tu Chin, Foreign Minister Pyun, Defense Minister Sohn Won Il, and an unresolved `Minister Kim`. Rhee argued for the Combined Economic Board as a supreme planning/coordinating body and objected to uncoordinated ECA/UNKRA procurement. Paik separately requested a meeting with Secretary Stevens to discuss immediate economic-aid problems. This supports distinct action edges for Rhee and Paik; it does not identify `Minister Kim` or prove that the other attendees endorsed every statement.

Source: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p2/d737
Mirror/context: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v14p2/d671

### 2. Korean procurement/Japan dispute
The parallel FRUS XIV/2 minutes show Rhee pressing to spend aid in Korea, objecting to Japanese procurement, and proposing that the CEB determine what had to be purchased in Japan. This is evidence of a Korean-side procurement-policy position, not evidence that Paik, Pyun, Sohn, Hwang Jong-ryul, or any specific procurement officer originated it.

Source: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v14p2/d671

### 3. Korean military programs, August 1953
FRUS XV/2 doc. 738 states that the Defense Minister summarized programs proposed by the three ROK armed services and that the Prime Minister requested U.S. approval. Attribution is therefore split: service programs -> summarized_by Sohn Won-il -> approval_requested_by Paik Tu Chin. The record does not by itself prove authorship of each service plan by Sohn or Paik.

Source: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p2/d738

### 4. 7–8 August consultation continuity
FRUS docs. 739–741 repeatedly list Paik, Pyun, Sohn, Ben C. Limb and `Minister Kim` alongside Rhee. Ben C. Limb is explicitly described as ROK Observer to the UN. `Minister Kim` remains unresolved and must not be merged with another Kim merely from title/name coincidence.

### 5. Yang You-chan and Pyo Wook Han as independent diplomatic actors
FRUS doc. 914 records Ambassador Yang delivering Rhee's signed letter on 7 July 1954 and stating that he had urged Rhee to make the Washington visit; Pyo Wook Han attended as Korean Embassy minister. This is an attributable diplomatic action by Yang and a verified office/context edge for Pyo, not evidence that Pyo authored the letter.

Source: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p2/d914
Additional provenance: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v14p2/d565

### 6. 1954 Washington delegation
FRUS docs. 925 and 927 list the Korean delegation: Rhee, Yang, Limb, Sohn Won-il, Paek Tu-chin, Choe Sun-chu, Chong Il-kwon, Kim Chong-yol, Choe Tok-sin. These are attendance edges only unless the minutes attribute speech/action to an individual.

Sources:
- https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p2/d925
- https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p2/d927

### 7. Won Yong Duk and NNSC attribution firewall
Doc. 927 records Dulles referring to General Won Yong Duk's warning to Communist NNSC members to leave the ROK. Doc. 930 reports uncertainty over whether Rhee had fulfilled a commitment to call on Won and recommends protest against ROK agents fostering disorders. Therefore: Won warning = supported; Rhee prior direct order for the warning = not established here; later demonstrations/violence = must not automatically be attributed to Won without additional evidence.

Sources:
- https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p2/d927
- https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p2/d930

### 8. Post-Washington NNSC policy continuity
FRUS doc. 941 says Robertson reminded Prime Minister Pyun in September 1954 that Rhee had agreed on 30 July to refrain from forceful action against the NNSC while the U.S. sought another solution. This creates a later diplomatic-policy edge for Pyun as prime minister/recipient but does not establish that Pyun controlled Won's earlier warning.

Source: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p2/d941

## Adversarial checks
1. Attendance is not endorsement.
2. Ministerial jurisdiction is not authorship.
3. Sohn summarized service plans; this does not prove he drafted them.
4. Paik requested U.S. approval; this does not prove he originated service requirements.
5. Rhee advocated CEB coordination; this does not prove Hwang Jong-ryul implemented the proposal.
6. `Minister Kim` remains unidentified.
7. Won's warning does not establish a Rhee direct-order chain.
8. NNSC demonstrations are not automatically attributable to Won.
9. Yang's delivery/advocacy does not make him author of Rhee's letter.
10. Pyo's attendance does not make him co-author of the letter.
11. 1954 delegation attendance does not imply agreement with every Rhee position.
12. Later offices of Chong/Kim/Choe must not be back-projected onto July 1954 without contemporaneous verification.
