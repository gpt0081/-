# Pass 130 — nursing/public-administration expansion and presidential approval edge

Date: 2026-09-25

## Material progress
This pass deliberately moved away from the already-mined 1954 scope-change proposal and followed two later institutional-expansion branches: nursing and public administration. The strongest new presidential edge is a University of Minnesota nursing-adviser final report. It states that the Department of Nursing, SNU College of Medicine, was officially established through President Rhee's approval on January 13, 1959. SNU's own nursing chronology independently records the 1959 conversion to a four-year department. The repository should therefore represent a presidential **approval** edge, but not silently inflate that edge into personal authorship of the nursing reform: the Korean decree/order, Ministry routing, cabinet context, and originating SNU proposal remain unrecovered.

The same contemporaneous report exposes a separable personnel chain. It says the Dean of the College of Medicine strongly supported reserving one of the new faculty positions allotted by the Ministry of Education for a Director of Nursing, and that in November 1959 the medical faculty elected 이귀향 (Lee Kwi-hyang), formerly principal of the technical nursing school, as director. University of Minnesota Archives independently documents Lee's six-month Minnesota nursing-training stay in 1957. This creates a useful action chain: presidential institutional approval → Ministry faculty allocation → dean support → faculty election → implementation by Lee, without collapsing those actors into one causal node.

A second branch confirms that public administration support was added to the Minnesota Project from 1957 and that SNU's Graduate School of Public Administration was founded on January 13, 1959. SNU 70-year history says the Korean government, U.S. government and joint economic-cooperation apparatus shared the perceived need for capable public administrators; SNU Law and GSPA histories independently anchor the school and date. The exact Korean proponents and approval instrument remain open, so no personal presidential authorship is assigned.

The fifth semiannual report yielded additional project-network nodes and concrete archival targets. It records Professor Hong Choon Ki's 1956 Smithsonian request for roughly 60 rock/mineral/ore specimens, delivered to SNU Mining Engineering on June 22, 1957; Korean Consul General Choo Young Han addressing Korean faculty/students in Minnesota on May 4, 1957; and the Korean Advisory Committee's January 30, 1957 review of ICA/Washington's proposed Amendment No.10. The fourth report independently anchors an October 10, 1956 committee meeting and ICA-proposed contract additions. These are retained as technical-assistance governance and diplomacy nodes, not presidential acts.

The December 8, 1954 photograph's unidentified Seoul High School principal 'Mr. Kim' was narrowed to a provisional Kim Won-gyu identity because a school chronology places 김원규 as principal from 1946 to March 1957. This is **not merged as verified** until an official school/Ministry roster or archival accession note confirms the full name. The unidentified Ministry of Education 'Dr. Park' likewise remains unmerged.
