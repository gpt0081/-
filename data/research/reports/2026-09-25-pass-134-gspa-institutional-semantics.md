# Research report — Pass 134 — 1959 GSPA institutional semantics

Date: 2026-09-25
Scope: follow Pass 133's founding-network work into the legal/institutional semantics of SNU GSPA, its Law College placement, first cohort, and 조사연구실. Floot was not modified or built.

## Material findings

1. **The founding event needs at least three dates/layers rather than one.** SNU GSPA's official history says the school was established by presidential decree in January 1959 **inside the College of Law**. Its official brochure gives the conventional founding date as **1959-01-13**, while the prior pass recovered **1959-04-01** as the general effective date of Presidential Decree No. 1429. These are not silently merged. The graph should retain promulgation/institutional-founding convention, legal effect, and operational opening as separate fields until the opening-semester records are recovered.

2. **Administrative independence came later.** SNU GSPA records that in **March 1961** the graduate school was separated from the College of Law. Therefore a model that treats the January 1959 institution as already administratively independent is too coarse.

3. **The first cohort is now bounded.** The official GSPA history records **80 first MPA graduates in March 1961**. Korea University's official profile independently confirms that 백완기 completed the SNU GSPA MPA in 1961 after graduating from SNU undergraduate study in 1959. This makes the 1959 admission register and March 1961 graduation register unusually high-yield targets: they can produce a finite early-state administrative network rather than a loose alumni list.

4. **The research-office layer belongs to the founding graph.** SNU Korea Institute of Public Affairs states that its predecessor **조사연구실** began when GSPA was created on 1959-01-13. KCI independently converges on a 1959 start. The SNU institute further says it became **행정조사연구소** under SNU Rule No. 95 on 1967-11-06. This supports a distinct 1959 research-office sub-event, but does not yet identify its first director, staff, budget, or internal establishment order.

5. **The Ministry-SNU personnel bridge deserves its own research line.** An SNU Archives biographical holding for a later GSPA dean records service as Ministry of Education Higher Education Bureau director in 1960 and GSPA dean in 1962-1965. This does not solve the 1958-1959 proposal chain, but it demonstrates the kind of personnel circulation that can connect Ministry files to university implementation and should be mined systematically.

## Attribution discipline

This pass deliberately does not convert institutional proximity into policy authorship. Presidential promulgation is not policy origination; ministerial jurisdiction is not proposal authorship; founding-professor status is not decree drafting; later institutional histories are not automatically contemporaneous legal instruments. Likewise, academics are not promoted to `data/politicians.json` merely because they were important to institution building.
