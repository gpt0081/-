# Pass 131 — 1959 SNU legal-routing crosscheck

Date: 2026-09-25

## Why this pass
Pass 130 left the 1959-01-13 nursing approval and public-administration expansion with a missing Korean legal/routing layer. This pass did not repeat the Minnesota discovery search. It moved upstream into the statute, SNU institutional history, responsible ministry, and dated officeholders.

## Findings

### 1. The 1959-01-13 date is an institutional reorganization date, not merely a nursing anecdote
SNU's university-wide official timeline records 1959-01-13 as the date on which nursing, diplomacy, Korean music, trade and other departments were newly established. SNU GSPA separately states that it was established on 1959-01-13 under the Education Act and SNU installation-order framework; its history page says the GSPA was created by presidential decree in January 1959 within the College of Law.

This materially strengthens the inference that the nursing approval reported by the Minnesota adviser belonged to a wider legal/administrative reorganization. It does **not** yet prove that nursing and GSPA were contained in one identical promulgation instrument. That remains an original-text question.

### 2. Historical statute text supplies the legal skeleton
The historical text of `국립학교설치령` marks the Seoul National University special-graduate-school clause as newly enacted on 1959-01-13 and marks the SNU central-organization provisions around Articles 8-10 as revised or wholly revised on the same date. The text assigns SNU academic-affairs administration responsibility over creation/abolition of colleges, departments and chairs, curricula, degrees, student quotas and academic research.

This is important for attribution. A presidential decree is the formal legal vehicle, but the administrative preparation and implementation ran through the education ministry and SNU machinery. Therefore the graph should not collapse `presidential decree` into `presidential policy authorship`.

### 3. 최재유 is the responsible cabinet education actor, but proposer/signatory status remains open
AKS identifies 최재유 (崔在裕, 1906-1993) as education minister from 1957, and contemporary archival material still identifies him as minister in 1960. His tenure therefore covers 1959-01-13. He is now a verified cabinet-level node for the institutional routing chain.

However, this pass did not recover the cabinet proposal sheet or signature page. The safe relation is `responsible portfolio holder / routing target`, not `proposed_by` or `drafted_by`.

A useful adversarial consequence follows: later 1961 judgment material concerning 최재유 and other Rhee cabinet ministers in the 3·15 election case exists in the National Archives/Democratization Memorial archive. That record is relevant to his later political responsibility but cannot be back-projected onto the 1959 education reorganization.

### 4. The 1959 medical-dean identity remains open; a 1955 anchor prevents a bad merge
An SNU College of Engineering article on the Minnesota Project reproduces a January 1955 Fowler photograph and explicitly captions 이제구 as SNU medical dean. This is a useful dated office anchor. It also reinforces the warning from Pass 130: 김동익's later SNU Hospital directorship is a different office and cannot be used to fill the 1959 medical-dean slot.

No reliable source opened in this pass proved that 이제구 remained dean in 1959. The node stays unresolved.

### 5. Public-administration institution has a firmer legal date
SNU GSPA's official pages now provide three mutually consistent institutional claims: establishment in 1959, establishment by presidential decree in January 1959, and a specific 1959-01-13 founding date under the Education Act/SNU installation framework. The SNU Archives authority record independently describes the GSPA as a professional graduate school established in 1959 under those legal bases. KCI records the associated research institute's establishment date as 1959-01-13 and its origin as the GSPA research office.

## Adversarial checks
1. Tested whether the Minnesota nursing report's 1959-01-13 presidential approval was an isolated claim: SNU official timeline independently matches the date.
2. Tested whether `presidential approval` implies presidential authorship: legal/administrative structure instead points to Ministry/SNU preparation and implementation; authorship remains unproven.
3. Tested whether 김동익 could be silently merged into the 1959 medical-dean node: office mismatch remains, so merge rejected.
4. Tested whether a dated medical dean could be found: 이제구 is verified for January 1955 only; continuity to 1959 rejected without evidence.
5. Tested whether GSPA's founding date was merely commemorative: official GSPA purpose/history, SNU Archives, and KCI institutional chronology converge on January 1959 / 1959-01-13.
6. Tested whether the legal change affected only graduate schools: historical statute text also marks SNU central administrative organization revisions on 1959-01-13.
7. Tested whether 최재유 can be labeled the specific proposer: tenure is verified but proposal/signature document absent, so attribution withheld.
8. Tested whether later political culpability records can establish 1959 education motives: rejected as temporal/subject-matter conflation.
9. Tested whether the current law-center search exposes the exact 1959 amendment text/decree number directly: not recovered in this pass; remains a document lead.
10. Tested whether GSPA and nursing necessarily share one instrument: same date and legal environment are strong clues, but exact identity of instrument remains insufficiently evidenced.
