## Findings

1. **명주완's role is materially stronger than Pass 132 recorded.** SNU Graduate School of Public Health's official former-deans page gives his first-dean term as **1959-04-01 to 1960-06-30**. That start date exactly matches Presidential Decree No. 1429's effective date. This upgrades him from a generic institutional-history lead to a verified first-dean/implementation actor. It still does not prove he proposed or drafted the decree.

2. **The GSPA actor network clearly predates/straddles formal legal creation.** The Korea Research Institute for Human Settlements' official biographical exhibition dates **노융희's SNU Graduate School of Public Administration professorship from 1958-08 to 1973-04**. Since Decree No. 1429 was promulgated 1959-01-13, this chronology is a high-value anomaly/lead: either a predecessor-unit appointment, retrospective institutional dating, or a pre-legal-implementation personnel arrangement. It must be resolved from SNU personnel orders rather than normalized away.

3. **유훈 is independently identified as a founding professor.** A 2026 Yonhap obituary records 1959-1994 GSPA professorship and explicitly describes him as a founding professor. This is good biographical evidence but not a substitute for the 1959 SNU appointment order.

4. **정인흥 belongs in the pre-institutional intellectual/organizational layer.** AKS records that he taught at SNU Law College through June 1959, published an early Korean public-administration textbook, and made substantial efforts toward creation of the SNU GSPA. This is evidence of institution-building, not proof of formal proposal authorship.

5. **The first-cohort layer can be reconstructed independently of elite-office records.** Korea University's official biography of 백완기 records SNU undergraduate graduation in 1959 and GSPA master's completion in 1961. An SNU emeritus-faculty memoir by 오석홍 states that he participated as a student when GSPA was created and became a first graduate. These sources can anchor the opening-semester roster/curriculum search.

6. **A 1959 GSPA research-office sub-event is now explicit.** KCI's institutional registry for the Korea Institute of Public Affairs dates a 조사연구실 inside GSPA to 1959, later expanded in 1967 and renamed in 1990. Exact establishment order, director, budget and staffing remain open.

## Attribution discipline

The new evidence makes the institutional network denser but does **not** move policy origination to any professor, dean or minister. This prevents the common error of treating a founding professor, first dean, minister with jurisdiction, and legal promulgator as interchangeable authors of the same policy.
