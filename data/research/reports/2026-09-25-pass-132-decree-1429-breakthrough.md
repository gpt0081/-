# Pass 132 — Presidential Decree No. 1429 legal-identity breakthrough

Date: 2026-09-25
Scope: follow Pass 131's highest-priority lead: identify the exact 1959-01-13 legal instrument behind the SNU institutional reorganization, then separate promulgation, effective date, policy authorship, and implementation.

## Main finding

The exact instrument is now identified from the National Law Information Center historical statute page: **국립학교설치령, 대통령령 제1429호, 1959-01-13 일부개정, 시행 1959-04-01**. Article 3 explicitly added two special graduate schools at Seoul National University: **행정대학원** and **보건대학원**. The statutory purpose text ties them to advanced university education and training personnel for administrative agencies and public-health work.

This resolves the narrow Pass 131 question about decree number and materially changes the graph. The two graduate schools are not merely parallel institutional-history dates. They share an express legal creation clause in the same presidential decree.

## Date correction and attribution discipline

Two dates must coexist:

- 1959-01-13: promulgation/amendment date; SNU GSPA institutional history also uses this as its establishment date.
- 1959-04-01: the decree's general statutory effective date under its supplementary provision.

These are not interchangeable. The repository should preserve `promulgated_on`, `institutional_founding_date`, and `effective_on` separately when the source traditions use different date semantics.

Likewise, Presidential Decree No. 1429 is strong evidence for **formal presidential legal action**, but not for `proposed_by` or `drafted_by`. Those fields remain open until cabinet agenda/minutes, the Ministry of Education proposal sheet, or SNU-to-Ministry correspondence is recovered.
