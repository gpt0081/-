## Actor findings

### Cho Chong-hwan (조정환 / 曺正煥)
The NIKH chronology independently fixes his office as Vice Foreign Minister on 25 October 1953 and his appointment to the Panmunjom preliminary delegation. Secondary biographical search gives the candidate normalization `曺正煥 / Cho Jeong-hwan`, 1892-12-10–1967-11-25, and a second vice-minister term beginning 1952-10-02. Because those biographical fields are not yet backed by an official personnel record in this pass, retain IDENTIFIED and mark normalization fields as provisional.

### Limb Ben / Yim Byeong-jik (임병직)
Direct 1953 correspondence now spans armistice messaging, economic-administration requests, public diplomacy, and diplomatic-network routing. This strengthens VERIFIED_PROPOSED, but exact official title/appointment dates still need official personnel normalization before stable migration.

### Pyun Yung-tai (변영태)
The evidence base now clearly spans POW-release explanation, treaty public diplomacy, press diplomacy, and an acting-prime-minister economic correspondence chain. Keep VERIFIED and add role edges rather than flattening these into 'Rhee policy'.

### Paik Too-chin (백두진)
The 8 July CEB letter is direct evidence of an independent economic-administration role. It explicitly references prior correspondence by Hanlon and acting PM Pyun, giving a multi-actor administrative chain rather than a presidential-only event.

### Paik Sun-yup (백선엽)
The 14 March ammunition statement is a direct Paik-origin record. Rhee's June/July correspondence with Yang concerning Paik is separate evidence of presidential perceptions/instructions and must not be treated as Paik's own position.

### Yang You-chan (양유찬)
Rhee→Yang records of 12 June and 2 July show Yang as a direct diplomatic recipient for military-command and armistice-sensitive issues. This supports independent actor treatment.

### Choi Duk-shin (최덕신)
The 19 March chronology establishes appointment as Korean armistice-talk representative. Candidate should be researched beyond military biography because this is a formal state-representation role; politician-registry eligibility remains OPEN pending broader office history.

### Cho Pyong-ok (조병옥)
The U.S. diplomatic index preserves an August 1953 opposition-politics conversation. It is valuable for network mapping but must be classified as contemporaneous foreign diplomatic observation/reporting, not a definitive account of his private motives.

### Shin Tae-ak (신태악)
The Liberal Party inspection chief's March statement on Chang Taek-sang/Sinrahoe provides a domestic party-organization node distinct from presidential administration.

### Chang Taek-sang (장택상)
The same record places Chang and Sinrahoe in a concrete party-entry conflict. This is a useful domestic-politics event outside the armistice/treaty cluster.

## Adversarial / attribution checks

1. `Rhee decided POW release = Pyun executed release` remains rejected. Pyun's letter reports/explains the presidential order; operational execution remains OPEN.
2. `Every document in the Rhee papers is authored by Rhee` is rejected by Limb, Son Pyong-sik, Oliver, Goodfellow, Francesca and others as senders.
3. `Every indexed person participated in the action` is rejected as a data rule; `le_005_1170`, `le_005_3200`, `le_005_2070` illustrate index-vs-authorship separation.
4. `1953 state policy can be represented as a Rhee-only graph` is contradicted by direct Paik Too-chin, Pyun, Paik Sun-yup, Limb and diplomatic-legation records.
5. `Political-conference representation is only Rhee/Limb/Pyun/Yang` remains contradicted by Cho Chong-hwan and Lee Soo-young's October appointment.
6. `Armistice representation begins only after the July armistice` is contradicted by Choi Duk-shin's 19 March appointment to armistice talks.
7. `Yang is merely an indexed ambassador name` is narrowed by direct Rhee→Yang correspondence on 12 June and 2 July.
8. `Paik Sun-yup's military position can be inferred from Rhee's descriptions of him` is rejected; direct Paik-origin ammunition statement must be separately modeled.
9. `Cho Pyong-ok's reported private views are established facts` is rejected; U.S. diplomatic reporting is contemporaneous observation and requires Korean/opposition-side corroboration.
10. `Liberal Party politics is reducible to presidential decisions` is contradicted by the Shin Tae-ak–Chang Taek-sang/Sinrahoe party-entry dispute, which requires independent party-actor edges.
