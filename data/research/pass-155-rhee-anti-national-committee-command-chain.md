# Pass 155 — Rhee / Special Committee for Investigation of Anti-National Activities command-chain audit

Date: 2026-09-26

## Material advance

This pass deliberately returned the research queue from the long teacher-labor-law genealogy to the active Rhee Syng-man backbone. A high-value command-attribution point is now pinned: the National Institute of Korean History chronology identifies 1949-06-07 as the date on which President Rhee publicly stated that the police dissolution of the Special Committee's special police unit was carried out on his order. This is materially stronger than merely attributing the June 6 raid to police actors or to the administration generally.

The attribution must remain narrow. It supports presidential responsibility for ordering the special-police-unit dissolution/disarmament. It does not, by itself, prove that Rhee personally scripted every arrest, search, seizure, assault, or local raid carried out on June 6.
