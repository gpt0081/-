# Rhee Pass 81 — KDB institutional lineage and actor leads

Date: 2026-09-22
Scope: ordinary economic/industrial governance. Follow-up to pass 80 Law No.302 lead. Floot untouched.

## Why this line
Pass 80 established the statutory design of Law No.302 but left the proposal/legislative/implementation actor chain open. This pass moves backward to the wartime drafting chronology and forward to the first executive leadership, without attributing every institutional act to the president.

## Documents/pages directly inspected
1. National Archives of Korea, `기록으로 만나는 대한민국 > 경제·산업 > 산업은행` — official archive presentation. It states drafting began in May 1951 in Busan, the bill was sent to the National Assembly in October 1951, deliberation was delayed during the war, it passed in November 1953, was promulgated in December, and KDB launched in April 1954 after organization and government capital payment.
2. National Law Information Center recent-promulgation listing for 1953-12-31 — official legal database, listing `한국산업은행법`, Law No.302, promulgated/effective 1953-12-30.
3. Bank of Korea official biography of first governor 구용서 — official institutional biography. It records 1950 BOK governor, 1953 Korea Coal Corporation president, 1954 first/second KDB governor, and 1958 commerce-industry minister.
4. Academy of Korean Studies, `한국산업은행` — secondary institutional synthesis independently repeats May/October 1951 drafting/submission, November 1953 passage and December promulgation, and describes KDB as a wholly government-capitalized public-law institution.
5. AKS, `민중자결단` — re-opened only for adversarial contrast: political mobilization and constitutional petitioning are a distinct governance channel from the KDB statutory/institutional chain; no causal merge is warranted.

## Findings
### 1. The KDB law was not a post-armistice invention
The National Archives chronology pushes the institutional design back to **May 1951**, with a government drafting process in wartime Busan and **October 1951 National Assembly referral**. Therefore the November/December 1953 enactment should be modeled as resumption/completion of a wartime policy line, not as a policy suddenly created after the armistice.

Attribution limit: the archive page says `정부` began drafting. It does not identify the individual drafter, minister, bureau chief, Cabinet proposer, committee rapporteur, or Rhee's personal drafting role. Those remain open.

### 2. Legislative completion and executive implementation must be separated
The same official archive presentation gives a useful four-stage sequence:
- 1951-05: government drafting begins;
- 1951-10: bill referred to National Assembly;
- 1953-11: National Assembly passage after wartime delay;
- 1953-12-30: Law No.302 promulgated;
- 1954-04: organization/capital completed and KDB launched.

This should become separate sub-events because proposed_by, committee review, plenary passage, promulgation, capitalization and executive appointments are legally and institutionally different actions.

### 3. 구용서 becomes a high-value non-president actor node
The Bank of Korea's official biography confirms **具鎔書 / 구용서** as first BOK governor (1950-06-05 to 1951-12-18), Korea Coal Corporation president in 1953, first/second KDB governor in 1954, and commerce-industry minister from 1958. This is a strong cross-institution bridge linking central banking, coal/industrial administration, development finance and later cabinet office.

Research status proposed: `VERIFIED`, but not yet `ANNALS_ACTIVE`. Exact KDB appointment date/instrument, nomination chain, and whether he participated in the 1951 bill drafting remain open.

### 4. Law No.302 actor decomposition remains necessary
The official legal listing independently fixes promulgation/effective date as 1953-12-30. Pass 80 already captured the law's government-capital design. The next critical task is to recover the original bill, National Assembly committee report and plenary minutes to identify the finance minister/bureau and legislators who proposed, reviewed, amended, supported or opposed it.

## Adversarial checks
1. **Claim:** KDB was created only after the armistice. **Counter-search result:** contradicted by National Archives chronology; drafting began May 1951 and referral occurred October 1951.
2. **Claim:** 1953 passage proves 1953 authorship. **Result:** contradicted; the bill had a wartime prehistory.
3. **Claim:** Rhee personally drafted Law No.302. **Result:** insufficient evidence; official archive attributes drafting generically to government.
4. **Claim:** promulgation equals bank launch. **Result:** contradicted; law promulgation was 1953-12-30, institutional launch followed in April 1954.
5. **Claim:** 구용서's KDB leadership alone proves he drafted the 1951 bill. **Result:** unsupported; BOK biography establishes offices, not bill authorship.
6. **Claim:** KDB was an ordinary private bank. **Result:** contradicted by Law No.302/AKS institutional description of government capitalization/public-law character.
7. **Claim:** November 1953 passage and December promulgation are the same event. **Result:** false; separate legislative/executive stages.
8. **Claim:** a later KDB governor list is sufficient for appointment provenance. **Result:** no; original appointment instrument remains required.
9. **Claim:** wartime delay explains all legislative inactivity. **Result:** official archive states wartime delay, but committee-level chronology and political causes still need primary minutes.
10. **Claim:** economic-governance evidence can be collapsed into the constitutional mobilization line from pass 80. **Result:** no; provenance and actor chains are distinct and should remain separate until a document connects them.
