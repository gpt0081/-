## Claim audit B: proposed economic constitutional amendment, narrow evidence
Gazette no.1049 transcription clearly identifies notice no.51 dated 1954-01-23, presidential seal line, cabinet members, and proposals concerning Articles 85, 87, 88, 89. This verifies **publication of a cabinet-approved proposal**, not legislative passage, cabinet members' individual votes, or executive implementation. The NIKH archive also indexes distinct withdrawal request/motion/approval records and later November amendments; these must be separately inspected before asserting withdrawal date or equivalence to the November enactment.

Editorial comparison has an additional textual issue requiring facsimile check: Article 89 description's old/new comparison appears to use Article 86 and private-enterprise nationalization in a way that differs from the transcribed notice, which specifies Article 86 for farmland and Article 88 for private enterprises. Also Article 88 editorial comparison writes 管轄 while the notice reads 管理. These are **transcription/editorial discrepancies to investigate**, not automatically errors in the 1954 gazette.
