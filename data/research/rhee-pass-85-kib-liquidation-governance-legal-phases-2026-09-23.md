# Rhee research pass 85 — KIB liquidation governance phases and statutory control

Date: 2026-09-23
Scope: Korea Industrial Bank liquidation governance, KDB establishment committee authority, actor-role chronology, and correction of phase conflation.

## Why this line matters
Pass 84 established that KIB dissolution, commercial-function succession, residual-property administration and long-tail liquidation were not one event. This pass tests a second hidden simplification: whether the same liquidator/governance arrangement can be projected across 1954-1963. It cannot.

8. Post-1960 Article 3: KDB governor became KIB liquidator; a liquidation committee was created for important liquidation matters.

## Material findings
### F85-1 — KDB establishment governance and KIB liquidation governance were legally separate
Decree 860 created a nine-member KDB Establishment Committee under a Finance-Minister-designated chair. The liquidation decree separately required post-dissolution shareholder action to select/recommend liquidator(s). Therefore `KDB establishment commissioner`, `KIB incumbent president`, `liquidator`, and later `KDB governor as statutory liquidator` are distinct role types and must not be collapsed.

### F85-2 — the 1960 governance rewrite is a hard chronological boundary
The currently searchable consolidated Article 3 says the KDB governor is liquidator and chairs an eight-person liquidation committee, but the text itself marks Article 3 as wholly revised on 1960-11-29. This arrangement cannot be cited as proof that Gu Yong-seo automatically held the legal liquidator office in April 1954. Pass 84 correctly retained the redacted Choi representative-liquidator candidate; this pass adds the legal reason why that is plausible rather than anomalous.

### F85-3 — Article 4 gives a concrete archival target for identifying Choi
The decree requires the KIB president to convene an extraordinary shareholders meeting within ten days after dissolution to recommend/select liquidator(s). The missing meeting notice, attendance/proxy list, minutes and resolution are therefore the highest-value primary records for identifying `최ㅇㅇ` and determining whether there were multiple liquidators or a representative liquidator chosen among them.

### F85-4 — Im Song-bon is a real KIB-president actor but does not solve `Hong`
Two independent biographical sources confirm Im Song-bon served as Korea Industrial Bank president. Because the official archive guide's April 1954 role is redacted as surname Hong, Im cannot be silently substituted. The presidency may have changed, the guide's role wording may differ, or another executive may be meant. Preserve both facts and recover the original instrument/1954 executive roster.

### F85-5 — Yu Wan-chang's chair claim becomes more clearly phase-sensitive
A secondary biography's statement that Yu Wan-chang chaired a KIB liquidation committee is not necessarily inconsistent with the April 1954 representative liquidator Choi. The legal regime changed over time and `liquidator`, `representative liquidator`, and `liquidation committee chair` are not guaranteed to be the same office. Exact appointment date and governing version remain open.

## Adversarial checks
1. Against `Gu Yong-seo was automatically KIB liquidator in 1954 because later Article 3 says KDB governor is liquidator`: rejected as anachronistic; Article 3 was wholly revised in 1960.
2. Against `Choi representative liquidator conflicts with the liquidation decree`: Article 4's shareholder-selection mechanism makes a separately selected liquidator structurally plausible.
3. Against `one continuous liquidation committee composition 1954-1963`: rejected; 1959 amendment and 1960 wholesale Article 3 revision require phase modeling.
4. Against `establishment committee = liquidation committee`: Decree 860 and Decree 859 create distinct functions and authority chains.
5. Against `nine establishment commissioners imply nine liquidation officials`: unsupported; separate legal objects.
6. Against `Im Song-bon must be the April 1954 KIB head`: biographical evidence proves he held the presidency at some point, not that he is the redacted Hong actor.
7. Against `Yu Wan-chang's later/undated chair role disproves Choi`: role type and phase differ; exact dates required.
8. Against `KIB dissolved on 1954-01-18 because liquidation decree was promulgated then`: Article 2 ties dissolution to KDB establishment day, not decree promulgation day.
9. Against `KIB liquidation ended with dissolution`: later statutory amendments and 1963 records disprove this.
10. Against `current consolidated text can be read as original 1954 text`: explicit amendment metadata disproves this methodology.
