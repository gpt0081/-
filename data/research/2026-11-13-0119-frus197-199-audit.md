## Findings and evidentiary limits

- **197:** Gross reported an informal June 27 UN dinner conversation with Zinchenko on Geneva Convention interpretation and possible POW arrangements. Gross explicitly recorded both parties describing their exchange as personal. His inference about a possible Soviet sounding-out is speculation, not authorization. Zinchenko's approximately 60,000 figure is his conversational assertion, not a verified census.
- **198:** Muccio wrote Johnson June 30 that he lacked information about Tokyo's contingency planning and feared a large American military government. He and Herren preferred, *if intervention became necessary and directives were issued*, to act through ROK military leadership while preserving Korean administration. This letter establishes a disagreement over contemplated mechanics, not a takeover. It also references the Combined Economic Board as a proposed institution, not proof of its operation.
- **199:** FRUS editors summarize Harrison's July 1 Panmunjom presentation: 62 of 63 draft paragraphs broadly settled, paragraph 51 POW repatriation unresolved. The editor identifies original telegrams C 51133, C 51166 and C 51282. This note is not counted as a contemporary primary transcript.
