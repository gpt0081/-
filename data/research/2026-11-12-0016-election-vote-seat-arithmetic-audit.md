## New source-integrity finding: votes versus seats
The 1954 election table presents a 203-seat total, Liberal 144, Democratic Nationalist 15, National Association 3, Korea Nationalist 3, independents 68. These displayed seat categories sum to 233, whereas the headline's Liberal 114 produces 203. This conflict was already identified in the previous note.

**Separate newly audited layer:** the same table's vote totals are internally consistent: 2,756,081 + 593,499 + 192,109 + 72,925 + 286,097 + 3,591,597 = **7,492,308**, exactly the published aggregate. Therefore the seat-count discrepancy must not be generalized into a claim that the entire table is corrupt. The 'other' vote column has 286,097 votes but the seat row shows a dash, a distinct representation question. Vote totals cannot validate seat totals because first-past-the-post outcomes are district-dependent.
