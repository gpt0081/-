# Pass 73 — Korean-side UN diplomacy chain, 1953

Access date: 2026-09-22

## Purpose

Pass 72 established the UN document lineage around armistice paragraph 60 and Resolution 711(VII), but Korean-side attribution remained thin. This pass pivots to the National Institute of Korean History (NIKH) `이승만서한철 / 이승만관계서한자료집 5(1953)` and treats the indexed letters as Korean archival objects rather than inferring Korean policy solely from UN/FRUS records.

## Findings

1. The NIKH 1953 volume itself is a high-value document registry. It exposes dated sender/title metadata for a long sequence of ROK diplomatic, military, economic, information and aid records. It should be mined as a reusable Korean provenance family rather than as isolated Rhee letters.
2. `le_005_2150`, 1953-07-01, is an 임병직 → 이승만 record titled `아시아-아랍권 대표들 접촉, 한국 지지 호소`. Its index explicitly names Asian-Arab delegates at the UN. This is direct Korean archival evidence that Limb was conducting coalition diplomacy before the August resumed General Assembly.
3. The same volume identifies an 임병직 record dated 1953-07-22 titled `유엔총회에서 한국문제 다룰 예정`. This creates a Korean-side bridge from the Robertson/armistice endgame into the later resumed General Assembly sequence.
4. The volume identifies an 임병직 record dated 1953-09-22 titled `소련의 정치회담 참여 주장 반대`, followed by Rhee → Limb on 1953-09-30 (`정치회담 참가국 선정 관련, 영국 비난`) and Limb → Rhee on 1953-10-06 (`인도의 정치회담 참가국 선정 관련`). This is a bilateral instruction/report chain, not merely a later memoir-level attribution.
5. `le_005_3310` indexes Lodge plus India, Burma, Pakistan and Indonesia and explicitly tags `political conference` and `neutralization`. Limb therefore merits promotion from a thin IDENTIFIED candidate toward VERIFIED for the 1953 political-conference diplomacy network, while the exact contents remain subject to image/text retrieval.
6. The volume continues the chain with Limb on 1953-11-11 (`공산당 잔학행위 유엔총회 의제 상정`), U.S. Embassy material on 1953-11-26 (`정치회담 참가국 선정 문제`), Dulles on 1953-12-01 (`정치회담 참가국 선정 협조 감사`), and Rhee → Yang You-chan on 1953-12-02 (`인도 등 정치회담 참가국 선정 반대`). Conference-composition diplomacy thus persists well beyond the 28 August Resolution 711 vote.
7. `le_005_3180`, 1953-09-02, is Oliver → Rhee couple, `정치회담에 대한 한국정부의 입장 건의`, indexed to Pyun Yung-tai, Dulles, Eisenhower and Yang You-chan, with India/Soviet Union and neutrality. It is a pointer, not proof that Pyun authored the policy. The Foreign Ministry outgoing record remains required.
8. The archive also contains Rhee → Limb on 1953-03-19 criticizing the India resolution and Limb → Rhee on 1953-06-10 asking for a statement against the new armistice proposal. This demonstrates continuity of the Rhee–Limb diplomatic channel before the August conference-composition fight.

## Adversarial attribution checks

- Do not equate a document indexed under `이승만서한철` with personal presidential authorship; sender fields are authoritative for attribution.
- Do not infer Pyun authorship from `le_005_3180`; Pyun appears as an indexed person in an Oliver letter.
- Do not infer that Limb personally drafted all ROK UN positions from his reporting chain; retrieve outgoing Foreign Ministry/Observer Mission instructions.
- Do not equate Rhee opposition to India with legal veto power at the UN. UN First Committee record 617 explicitly distinguishes Korea's wishes from a veto.
- Do not collapse July coalition lobbying, August UNGA composition voting, September–October participation disputes, and November–December conference arrangements into one event.
- Do not infer a direct causal line from Rhee's 9/30 letter to the 10/6 Limb report until full images/text establish the response relation.
- Do not treat Dulles's 12/1 thanks as proof of full ROK-US agreement; Rhee's 12/2 opposition to India and other participants shows continuing dispute.
- Do not treat later political-conference diplomacy as a continuation of the armistice signature itself; model separate event/action nodes linked by paragraph 60.
- Do not treat the NIKH metadata index as equivalent to full-text verification where the image has not been opened.
- Do not downgrade UN/FRUS evidence when Korean records differ; preserve provenance-specific observations and conflicts.
