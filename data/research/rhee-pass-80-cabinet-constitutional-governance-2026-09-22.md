## New findings

### A. 1953-05-16 cabinet record supplies direct presidential constitutional-policy attribution
The National Archives cabinet-minutes presentation identifies the source as the 42nd cabinet meeting held at the presidential residence on 1953-05-16. Its thirteenth presidential instruction ordered the government to press the National Assembly on a referendum system, recall of National Assembly members, Councillors election, overseas exhibition of national treasures, and abolition of priority in public sale of vested property. The archive separately summarizes Rhee's constitutional position that matters concerning national sovereignty should be decided by popular vote.

Attribution rule: this record supports `proposed_by / instructed_by = Rhee` for pressing these agenda items. It does **not** establish enactment, National Assembly acceptance, or authorship of a completed constitutional amendment.

### B. The recall proposal has an extra-government mobilization edge, but causal transmission remains open
The AKS entry on 민중자결단 records that on 1953-06-09 the organization issued a petition to the National Assembly demanding the National Assembly-member recall system advocated by Rhee and a constitutional amendment granting wartime emergency powers to the president. This is useful as a separate organization/action node one month after the cabinet instruction. It does not by itself prove that the May 16 cabinet instruction directly caused the June 9 petition, or that the government drafted the petition.

Organization consequence: create/prepare a stable organization node for `org-minjung-jagyeoldan` before attributing its collective action to any individual leader. AKS identifies 백성욱 and 윤재관 among founders/organizers, but exact responsibility for the 1953-06-09 text remains an open lead.

### C. 손원일's defense-minister transition now has Korean official-archive chronology
The National Archives Korean War timeline records `1953-06-30 국방부장관 손원일 임명`. This independently strengthens the transition date already inferred from biographical material and FRUS attendance. It is still a chronology page citing military-history works, not the appointment instrument itself, so the gazette/appointment record remains the preferred primary target.

### D. 1953 ordinary governance has a concrete institutional-finance design
The original Korean Development Bank Act, Law No. 302, promulgated/effective 1953-12-30, states that the bank's principal purpose was to finance/manage funds for important industries in line with national policy, set capital at 400 million hwan wholly subscribed by the government, required an initial one-quarter payment, and made later payments subject to Cabinet decision. This creates a cabinet-level industrial-finance governance edge that should not be reduced to presidential authorship.

### E. Keep source-family independence strict
The Korean History DB FRUS translation is useful for actor identification but is not an independent Korean-origin provenance family against U.S. FRUS. Conversely, the National Archives cabinet-minute record is Korean-origin government provenance and can be cross-checked against later constitutional/mobilization evidence without collapsing the source families.
